# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SOLITARY_PLASMACYTOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
By definition no evidence of systemic myeloma (classically a solitary biopsy-proven lesion with normal skeletal survey/advanced imaging except for the primary lesion, no myeloma-defining events/CRAB attributable to plasma cell disorder, and bone marrow showing no or minimal clonal plasma cells; current criteria generally allow <10% clonal marrow plasma cells if no other lesions are present). Progression to MM: ~50-70% of SBP and ~10-30% of EMP over 10 years. Treated with local RT.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Progression to MM: ~50-70% of SBP over 10 years; ~10-30% of EMP. RT (typically 40-50 Gy) is standard local therapy. Persistent M-protein after RT is an adverse predictor of MM progression. EMP has better long-term prognosis than SBP.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same plasma cell lineage biology as MM but localised. SBP progression to MM more common than EMP — reflects marrow-niche dependency of plasma cell biology. EMP at upper aerodigestive tract sites may reflect distinct mucosal plasma cell lineage. RT-curable when truly solitary; persistent M-protein after RT is an adverse predictor of MM progression.

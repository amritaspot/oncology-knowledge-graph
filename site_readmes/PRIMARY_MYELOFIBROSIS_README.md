# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PRIMARY_MYELOFIBROSIS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
JAK2/CALR/MPL driver mutations in most cases; additional high-molecular-risk mutations are common. Allogeneic HSCT is the only curative option; JAK inhibitors are standard for symptom and splenomegaly control.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Only curative therapy is allogeneic HSCT. Ruxolitinib is standard for symptoms and splenomegaly (COMFORT-I/II). Momelotinib is especially useful in anaemic patients (also inhibits ACVR1, modulating hepcidin). Pacritinib is an option for severe thrombocytopenia. Fedratinib is an approved JAK2 inhibitor option, including after ruxolitinib failure/intolerance.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Progressive fibrosis is driven by inflammatory cytokines produced by clonal cells and reactive stromal cells (fibroblasts are generally reactive rather than part of the malignant clone). Extramedullary haematopoiesis in spleen/liver reflects reactivation of fetal haematopoietic sites. Only curative therapy is allogeneic HSCT.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SINONASAL_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Regional cervical nodal metastases are less common at presentation than in other head and neck SCCs; distant metastases occur mainly in advanced/recurrent disease. The note 'See ORAL_CAVITY_SCC — aggregated UAT data from Riihimäki 2018 applies' is not anatomically site-specific for sinonasal SCC and should not be used as a sinonasal metastasis pattern source.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TME parallels other head and neck SCCs, but sinonasal carcinomas are biologically heterogeneous and include distinct molecular entities.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Occupational exposures are recognized risk factors, classically wood dust and leather dust for sinonasal malignancies overall; nickel exposure is also established in sinonasal carcinogenesis. HPV can be detected in a subset of sinonasal SCC, particularly non-keratinizing tumors, but prevalence and prognostic significance vary by series. EGFR exon 20 insertions are a hallmark driver in inverted papilloma-associated sinonasal SCC. SMARCB1-deficient variant needs specific IHC (INI-1 loss) to identify.

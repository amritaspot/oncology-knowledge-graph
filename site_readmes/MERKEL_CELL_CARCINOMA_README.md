# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MERKEL_CELL_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive cutaneous neuroendocrine carcinoma; high regional nodal metastasis rate; distant spread commonly to skin/body wall (including in-transit/distant cutaneous metastases), liver, bone, lung, and brain.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Two distinct pathogenic pathways with distinct TMB and antigen profiles — but both can be highly immunotherapy-responsive (avelumab was the first ICI approved specifically for this cancer). Immunocompromised patients have worse outcomes.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Cell of origin debate ongoing — despite name, may not arise directly from mature Merkel mechanoreceptor cells. Two distinct pathways both produce immunotherapy-responsive disease through different mechanisms (viral antigens vs UV neoantigens). CK20 perinuclear dot pattern is diagnostically useful. Distinct from lung small cell carcinoma despite morphologic similarity.

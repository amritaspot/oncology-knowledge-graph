# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CUTANEOUS_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Overall low metastatic rate (~2-5%); risk is substantially higher in high-risk lesions (e.g., immunosuppressed/transplant recipients, deeply invasive/thick tumors, poor differentiation, perineural invasion, ear/lip location, recurrent tumors). Regional lymph nodes are the most common first site, with distant spread most often to lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Extremely high TMB (UV) drives IO responses — cemiplimab and pembrolizumab are both effective. High-risk features (depth, perineural invasion, immunosuppression) drive adjuvant decisions. Transplant recipients need enhanced surveillance.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same squamous lineage biology as SCC of other sites (head/neck, lung, esophagus, cervix) — p63/p40/SOX2 lineage circuit. UV-driven biology explains extreme TMB and IO responsiveness. Solid organ transplant recipients have markedly increased risk from chronic immunosuppression; heavy screening burden.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: T_ALL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
T-lymphoblastic lymphoma and T-ALL are the same disease with different presentation (mass vs marrow). Mediastinal mass may cause SVC syndrome or airway compromise at presentation.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
T-lymphoblastic lymphoma and T-ALL are the same disease with different presentation. Mediastinal mass may cause SVC syndrome or airway compromise at presentation. Nelarabine is active in T-lineage disease, especially R/R disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
T-lymphoblastic lymphoma and T-ALL are same disease with different presentation (marrow-dominant vs mediastinal-dominant). NOTCH1 pathway is central to normal T-cell development and mutation drives leukaemogenesis. Nelarabine shows selective activity in T-lineage disease. Mediastinal mass may cause SVC syndrome / airway compromise.

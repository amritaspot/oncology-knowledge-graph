# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: INFLAMMATORY_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
See INVASIVE_DUCTAL_NST — aggregate breast data applies. IBC has a high metastatic rate at diagnosis (~20–30% in contemporary series); dermal lymphatic invasion is characteristic; higher brain/CNS metastasis risk is reported, particularly in HER2-positive and TNBC disease.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Aggressive with high metastatic rate at presentation. Requires trimodality therapy; breast-conserving surgery is generally not recommended, and omission of mastectomy or RT compromises outcome.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Clinicopathologic entity rather than distinct lineage — defined by rapid-onset inflammatory breast changes with frequent dermal lymphovascular tumour emboli producing the inflammatory clinical picture. Any intrinsic subtype can present this way but HER2-positive and TNBC are over-represented. Aggressive biology requires trimodality therapy sequence.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: B_PROLYMPHOCYTIC_LEUKAEMIA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Very high lymphocyte counts with prolymphocyte morphology; splenomegaly without significant lymphadenopathy is typical; aggressive. In modern classifications, many historical cases are now reclassified as prolymphocytic transformation of CLL/SLL, leukemic mantle cell lymphoma, or splenic B-cell lymphoma/leukemia with prominent nucleoli.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Very rare; modern classification now questions whether primary B-PLL is a distinct entity, and many cases are reclassified as CLL transformation, mantle cell lymphoma, or splenic B-cell lymphoma/leukemia with prominent nucleoli. TP53 alterations are common. Targeted approaches such as BTK inhibitors and venetoclax have been used, especially in TP53-aberrant disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Modern classification questions primary B-PLL as a distinct entity — many cases represent prolymphocytic transformation of CLL, leukemic mantle cell lymphoma, or splenic B-cell lymphoma/leukemia with prominent nucleoli. Distinguished from typical CLL by prolymphocyte percentage and brighter CD20/sIg, although immunophenotypic overlap exists. Exclusion of mantle cell lymphoma is essential.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ANAL_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
HPV-driven biology. Locoregional lymphatic spread dominates: inguinal (lower canal / perianal) and pelvic (perirectal/mesorectal, internal iliac/obturator; upper canal) nodes depending on anatomic level. Distant metastasis is less common than in colorectal adenocarcinoma; when it occurs, liver and lung are most common, with distant nodal spread also seen. Not separately reported in Riihimäki 2018 CRC data (which excludes anal SCC).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
HPV-driven biology parallels cervical and HPV+ oropharyngeal cancer. CRT (Nigro protocol) is standard; watch-and-wait after CRT is increasingly used for complete responders.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same HPV biology as cervical, HPV+ oropharyngeal, penile, and vulvar SCC — treated similarly with radiation-based approaches exploiting HPV-associated treatment sensitivity. Higher risk in HIV+ population. Anal Pap screening in high-risk populations is emerging.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ANGIOSARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive; wide dissemination — lung, liver, bone, and regional/distant soft tissue/skin are common metastatic sites; spleen and other viscera can occur. Cutaneous angiosarcoma of scalp/face and radiation-induced angiosarcoma have distinct clinical contexts.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Post-radiation (breast RT, MYC-amplified) is a distinct entity from primary sporadic. Cutaneous scalp variant of elderly can show UV-signature/high TMB — pembrolizumab active in a subset. Aggressive with poor overall prognosis.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Endothelial lineage explains vascular architecture (though often abortive). MYC amplification is characteristic of post-radiation angiosarcoma (breast RT) and also seen in some post-lymphedema cases — distinct from primary sporadic. Cutaneous scalp variant of elderly can have UV-signature/high TMB, which may help explain pembrolizumab activity in a subset. Aggressive with poor overall prognosis regardless of variant.

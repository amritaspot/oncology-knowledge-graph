# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SALIVARY_MUCOEPIDERMOID

### metastasis_patterns_note
No population-based source harvested. Clinical: low-grade rarely metastasizes; high-grade more often metastasizes, most commonly to regional cervical lymph nodes, and in distant spread to lung and bone.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TME not well-studied in salivary tumours. High-grade tumours may show more inflammation.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Most common malignant salivary gland tumor. MAML2-fusion status generally correlates with lower grade and better prognosis. High-grade transformation may lose characteristic mixed differentiation.

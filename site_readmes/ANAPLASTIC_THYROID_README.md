# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ANAPLASTIC_THYROID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Extremely aggressive; local invasion of trachea/oesophagus dominates; distant metastasis to lung common, with bone and brain also reported; median survival is typically measured in months.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Inflamed TME supports IO trials. Rapid local growth compresses airway/oesophagus — a surgical/airway emergency. BRAF+MEK combination is transformative for BRAF V600E-mutant subset.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Loss of thyroglobulin and TTF-1 is common in fully dedifferentiated tumours, but PAX8 is often retained in ATC and can help support thyroid origin. BRAF+MEK inhibition can partially 'redifferentiate' some thyroid cancers, but restoration of meaningful radioiodine uptake is not an established feature of ATC. Preserved BRAF V600E in some ATC allows dabrafenib+trametinib (transformative for that subset).

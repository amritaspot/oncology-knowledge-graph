# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SINONASAL_UNDIFFERENTIATED

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive sinonasal carcinoma with frequent local invasion and risk of regional nodal and distant metastasis; distant spread most commonly involves lung, bone, and liver.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Aggressive with rapid local invasion. TME under-studied. IDH2-mutant subset may have distinct biology.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
SNUC is being progressively subdivided as molecular markers identify specific entities (IDH2-mutant, SMARCB1-deficient, NUT carcinoma). Aggressive with poor prognosis; multimodal therapy required.

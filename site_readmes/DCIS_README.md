# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: DCIS

### metastasis_patterns_note
In situ disease; by definition non-invasive and non-metastatic. If untreated, some lesions progress to invasive breast cancer over time; precise risk is heterogeneous and depends on pathologic and clinical features rather than a single consensus 10-year figure.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Intact basement membrane defines in situ status. Progression to invasive disease requires breaching of the myoepithelial-basement membrane barrier.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
IHC panel for myoepithelium (p63, calponin, SMMHC) confirms intact layer. Some DCIS progresses to invasive disease; others do not — active area of research to identify which lesions require treatment vs surveillance.

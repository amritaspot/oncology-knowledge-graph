# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NSCLC_SARCOMATOID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive NSCLC variant with early distant spread; common metastatic sites include brain, bone, adrenal glands, liver, and contralateral lung/pleura, broadly similar to other advanced NSCLCs.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Aggressive but often immunotherapy-responsive. High rate of MET exon 14 alteration makes MET-directed therapy particularly relevant.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
MET exon 14 skipping alterations are enriched in this histology (~20-30%) and can confer sensitivity to tepotinib and capmatinib. PD-L1 expression is frequently high, and TMB may be elevated, which may contribute to immunotherapy benefit.

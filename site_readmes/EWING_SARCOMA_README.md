# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: EWING_SARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Paediatric/AYA. Metastatic sites: lung (~50%), bone/bone marrow (~25-35% each; together ~40-45%), less commonly regional lymph nodes. Bone marrow disease at diagnosis is a critical prognostic feature. EWSR1-FLI1 fusion.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
CIC-DUX4 and BCOR-CCNB3 fusion tumours were previously called 'Ewing-like' but are now distinct WHO entities with different behaviour. Multiagent chemotherapy (VDC/IE) is highly effective for localised disease; metastatic and relapsed disease more challenging.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Fusion oncoprotein EWSR1-FLI1 drives unique transcriptional programme through GGAA microsatellite enhancer creation. CD99 diffuse membranous pattern is characteristic lineage marker. CIC-DUX4 and BCOR-CCNB3 fusion sarcomas were previously called 'Ewing-like' but are now separate WHO entities with distinct biology and different behaviour.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ACRAL_MELANOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
See CUTANEOUS_MELANOMA. Distinct biology (BRAF-wt more common, KIT-mutant subset); regional nodal involvement is common at presentation, but the overall pattern of distant spread is broadly similar to other cutaneous melanomas.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinct biology from sun-exposed cutaneous melanoma — KIT alterations enriched, BRAF less frequent, and copy-number/structural alterations are prominent. Regional nodal metastasis is common at diagnosis. Amplifications are more common than UV-signature point mutations. Often diagnosed late due to atypical location.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct biology from sun-exposed cutaneous melanoma despite same lineage — anatomic site (acral) is associated with a different mutational landscape (KIT enriched, BRAF less common, not UV-driven). Regional nodal involvement is common at diagnosis. Same neural crest melanocyte origin but distinct clinical entity.

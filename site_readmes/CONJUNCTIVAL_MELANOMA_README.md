# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CONJUNCTIVAL_MELANOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Biology is generally closer to cutaneous melanoma than to other mucosal melanomas, with UV-signature tumors reported. Regional lymphatic spread commonly involves preauricular/parotid, submandibular, and cervical nodes; distant metastases may involve lung, liver, brain, and bone.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Biologically closer to cutaneous melanoma than mucosal or uveal — higher BRAF rate and more frequent UV-signature features than other mucosal melanomas. Wide excision + cryotherapy are standard local treatments. Immunotherapy has clinical activity. Regional nodal metastasis may involve preauricular/parotid, submandibular, and cervical nodes.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Anatomic position at conjunctival surface allows UV exposure, helping explain UV-signature mutations unlike many other mucosal sites. Biologically closer to cutaneous than uveal melanoma and closer to cutaneous than most non-conjunctival mucosal melanomas. Same neural crest lineage as other melanomas but distinct site-specific biology. Primary acquired melanosis (PAM) with atypia is a recognized precursor.

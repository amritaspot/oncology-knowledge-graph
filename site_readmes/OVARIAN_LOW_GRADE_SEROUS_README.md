# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: OVARIAN_LOW_GRADE_SEROUS

### metastasis_patterns_note
See OVARIAN_HIGH_GRADE_SEROUS — Riihimäki aggregate applies. LGSC is more indolent, peritoneal-dominant, and has less frequent visceral/distant spread than HGSC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Indolent but relatively chemoresistant. MEK inhibitor (trametinib) is a standard systemic option for recurrent LGSC. Hormonal therapy (for example, letrozole) also has activity.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct biology from HGSC despite the same anatomic site — characterized by frequent MAPK pathway activation (for example, KRAS, BRAF, NRAS) and relative absence of TP53 mutation/HRD typical of HGSC. This biology underlies sensitivity to MEK inhibition in recurrent disease and supports endocrine therapy use. Indolent with slower progression than HGSC.

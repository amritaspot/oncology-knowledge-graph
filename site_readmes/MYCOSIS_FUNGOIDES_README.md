# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MYCOSIS_FUNGOIDES

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Most common CTCL. Indolent early stages; skin-directed therapy (topical steroids, phototherapy, topical chemotherapy). Advanced disease: systemic therapy including brentuximab (CD30+), mogamulizumab (CCR4), bexarotene.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Most common CTCL. Indolent early stages managed with skin-directed therapy (topical steroids, phototherapy, topical chemo). Advanced disease may require systemic therapy: brentuximab (CD30+), mogamulizumab (CCR4), bexarotene, HDAC inhibitors. Large-cell transformation portends worse outcome.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Skin-homing memory T-cell lineage explains cutaneous tropism and the skin-directed therapy paradigm. CCR4 expression is common but not lineage-defining in every case and may support mogamulizumab activity. Epidermotropism with Pautrier microabscesses reflects lineage biology. Large-cell transformation with frequent CD30 expression represents progression and may enable brentuximab therapy.

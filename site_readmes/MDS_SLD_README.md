# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MDS_SLD

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
BM dysplasia + peripheral cytopenia in a single lineage. Indolent form. Not a metastatic disease — clonal marrow disorder with progression risk to AML.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Indolent lower-risk form with relatively low AML progression risk compared with higher-risk MDS. Supportive care (transfusions, ESAs) is often sufficient. Progression to higher-risk MDS or AML is the main long-term concern. IPSS-M molecular scoring refines risk beyond IPSS-R.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Represents an early lower-risk form of MDS with single-lineage dysplasia. HSC-origin clonal haematopoiesis is defining biology. Autoimmune complications and inflammatory dysregulation are increasingly recognised. IPSS-M molecular scoring refines risk beyond IPSS-R by incorporating driver mutation profile.

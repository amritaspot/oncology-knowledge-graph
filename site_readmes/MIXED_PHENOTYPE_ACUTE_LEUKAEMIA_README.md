# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MIXED_PHENOTYPE_ACUTE_LEUKAEMIA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Rare; blasts express both myeloid and lymphoid markers. Treatment approach varies, but contemporary retrospective series and guidelines generally favor ALL-like induction over AML-like therapy; outcomes remain inferior to standard ALL/AML, and allogeneic transplant is often considered in remission.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rare and heterogeneous. ALL-like induction is generally preferred over AML-like induction in many modern series, though treatment is individualized. Distinct molecular subgroups such as BCR::ABL1 and KMT2A-rearranged disease can guide specific approaches. Outcomes are generally poor relative to standard ALL/AML, and transplant is often considered in appropriate patients.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Rare and heterogeneous — thought to arise from an early progenitor or from leukemic transformation with lineage infidelity. Molecular subgroups can guide therapy: BCR::ABL1 warrants TKI therapy; KMT2A-rearranged disease is a target for emerging menin inhibitor strategies. ALL-like induction is often favored, with allogeneic transplant considered in suitable patients.

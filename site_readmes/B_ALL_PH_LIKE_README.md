# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: B_ALL_PH_LIKE

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Gene expression profile similar to Ph+ ALL but BCR-ABL1 negative. Heterogeneous kinase-activating drivers, including CRLF2 rearrangements with JAK-STAT lesions, ABL-class fusions, EPOR rearrangements, and JAK2 rearrangements. Adverse risk; targeted therapy may be considered according to the underlying alteration/fusion partner.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Molecular subtypes have distinct therapeutic implications: ABL-class fusions may benefit from TKIs; CRLF2/JAK-pathway lesions are being studied with ruxolitinib. Adverse risk overall; MRD-directed intensification is often considered in high-risk management.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Molecular heterogeneity with kinase-activating alterations parallel to BCR-ABL1 drives a similar gene expression signature. Molecular subtyping can direct targeted therapy: ABL-class fusions may be treated with TKIs (for example dasatinib or imatinib); CRLF2/JAK-pathway lesions are being studied with ruxolitinib. Adverse risk overall; MRD-directed intensification is often considered in high-risk management.

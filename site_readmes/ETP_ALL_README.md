# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ETP_ALL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Immature T-lineage acute lymphoblastic leukemia with stem cell/myeloid immunophenotype overlap. Historically considered adverse risk, though outcomes have improved with contemporary intensive T-ALL therapy and MRD-directed approaches; routine AML-directed therapy is not established standard of care.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Bridging phenotype between T-ALL and stem cell/myeloid-like leukemia, with enrichment for myeloid/stem-cell-associated mutations (including FLT3, RAS, DNMT3A, IDH1/2 in subsets). Relative BCL2 dependence has motivated venetoclax + chemotherapy studies, but this is not yet definitive standard therapy. Historically adverse; modern MRD-directed approaches have improved outcomes.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Reflects a unique developmental state: an immature T-cell precursor before full T-lineage maturation that retains stem/myeloid biological features. This helps explain overlap with myeloid-type mutational programs and interest in venetoclax-based approaches, but AML-directed therapy is not routine standard solely on this basis. Historically adverse; MRD-directed approaches and contemporary intensive T-ALL regimens have improved outcomes.

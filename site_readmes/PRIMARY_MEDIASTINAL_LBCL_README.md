# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PRIMARY_MEDIASTINAL_LBCL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Young adults, female predominance. Bulky anterior mediastinal mass. Related to cHL biology. Excellent prognosis with DA-EPOCH-R; R-CHOP has historically often been combined with mediastinal RT.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Young adults (median ~35), female predominance. Excellent prognosis with DA-EPOCH-R; R-CHOP has historically often been combined with mediastinal RT. CD30 expression may enable brentuximab vedotin activity in R/R disease. Relapse pattern is distinctive — extranodal (kidney, adrenal, GU, liver), less nodal.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Thymic B-cell origin distinguishes from typical DLBCL and explains shared biology with classical Hodgkin lymphoma. 9p24.1 alterations affecting PD-L1/PD-L2 are shared with cHL and help explain pembrolizumab activity (KEYNOTE-013/-170). CIITA rearrangements can cause reduced MHC-II expression disrupting antigen presentation. Young adults with female predominance. Distinctive extranodal relapse pattern reflects distinct biology.

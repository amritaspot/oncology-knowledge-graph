# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CHL_NODULAR_SCLEROSIS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Most common cHL subtype; young adults; mediastinal predominance; generally favorable prognosis. Extranodal spread in advanced disease can involve spleen, liver, bone marrow, and lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Most common cHL subtype (~60-70%). Young adults, mediastinal predominance. SWOG S1826 showed strong efficacy for nivolumab + AVD compared with brentuximab vedotin + AVD. PET-adapted therapy (RATHL) allows chemotherapy reduction in interim PET-negative disease. Long-term survivorship focus (cardiac, secondary malignancy).

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Reed-Sternberg cells are 'crippled' germinal centre B cells that lost B-cell identity but escaped apoptosis. Cellular hierarchy is unusual — RS cells <1% of tumour, with vast dysregulated inflammatory infiltrate. Frequent 9p24.1 alteration explains immune-checkpoint responsiveness. Nodular sclerosis subtype has broad collagen bands producing distinctive architecture.

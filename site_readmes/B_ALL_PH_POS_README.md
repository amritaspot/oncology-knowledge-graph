# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: B_ALL_PH_POS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
See B_ALL. Historically adverse risk; TKI (imatinib, dasatinib, ponatinib) plus chemo/immunotherapy transformed outcomes. CNS and testicular sanctuary sites remain clinically relevant; CNS prophylaxis is standard.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TKIs transformed outcomes from historically adverse to markedly improved. Chemo-free regimens (D-ALBA — dasatinib + blinatumomab) achieve high complete remission and MRD-negative rates. Ponatinib is an important adult frontline TKI option. T315I mutation confers resistance to most TKIs except ponatinib.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same BCR-ABL1 kinase biology as CML but arising in a B-lymphoid progenitor rather than a multipotent myeloid stem/progenitor context — cell of origin helps determine clinical phenotype. TKI-based therapy transformed outcomes. Chemo-free regimens (D-ALBA — dasatinib + blinatumomab) achieve high MRD-negative remission rates.

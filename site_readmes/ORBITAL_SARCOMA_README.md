# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ORBITAL_SARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Heterogeneous group; rhabdomyosarcoma is the commonest paediatric orbital sarcoma. Behaviour follows underlying sarcoma histology; see SOFT_TISSUE_AND_BONE_SARCOMA. Orbital rhabdomyosarcoma is typically a favourable-site RMS and commonly presents as localized disease; distant metastases at presentation are uncommon, and when present follow RMS patterns (especially lung, bone/bone marrow, and distant lymph nodes depending on subtype).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Orbital rhabdomyosarcoma has best RMS prognosis (favourable site). Anatomic constraints often preclude wide margins — multimodal therapy with vision preservation is the goal.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Category comprises multiple sarcoma entities occurring at orbital site. Orbital rhabdomyosarcoma has best RMS prognosis (favourable site classification). Cross-reference to RHABDOMYOSARCOMA entries under SOFT_TISSUE_AND_BONE_SARCOMA for lineage details. Anatomic constraints of orbit often preclude wide margins.

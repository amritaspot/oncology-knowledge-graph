# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: UNDIFFERENTIATED_PLEOMORPHIC_SARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Lung-dominant metastasis; less commonly bone, liver.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
SARC028 showed pembrolizumab activity — UPS is among the sarcoma subtypes most responsive to immune checkpoint blockade. Tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) have been associated with response. Category has narrowed as molecular reclassification identifies specific entities.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Diagnosis of exclusion — molecular classification progressively narrows what remains 'undifferentiated'. Genomically complex with no defining single recurrent alteration or pathognomonic fusion. Paradoxically one of the more immune-responsive sarcomas in clinical trials such as SARC028; TLS may enrich for response.

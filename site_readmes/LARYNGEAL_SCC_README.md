# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LARYNGEAL_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Regional spread is primarily to cervical lymph nodes; supraglottic primaries have substantially higher nodal metastasis rates than glottic primaries, while distant metastases most often involve lung, followed by bone and liver. Aggregated UAT data from Riihimäki 2018 may be used cautiously but site-specific laryngeal patterns differ from oral cavity SCC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Smoking-driven biology dominates. Verrucous variant has minimal invasion and better prognosis. TME parallels other smoking-related head and neck SCC.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Laryngeal papillomatosis (HPV 6/11) is a distinctive but usually benign precursor lesion, with malignant transformation being uncommon. Smoking cessation reduces risk of second primary. Preservation of laryngeal function is major therapy consideration.

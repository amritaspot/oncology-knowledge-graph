# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: HIGH_GRADE_B_CELL_LYMPHOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
MYC + BCL2 (with or without BCL6) rearrangements define HGBL with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements in WHO/ICC classifications. Aggressive; poorer outcomes with standard R-CHOP have been reported, and intensified regimens (for example DA-EPOCH-R) are often used, although an optimal standard regimen is not definitively established.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
WHO 2022 requires MYC + BCL2 for HGBL with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements diagnosis; cases with concurrent BCL6 rearrangement are included as triple-hit. MYC + BCL6 without BCL2 is no longer part of this entity and is generally classified as DLBCL, NOS or HGBL, NOS depending on morphology. DA-EPOCH-R is often used over R-CHOP in practice, but there is no universally established standard. CNS prophylaxis is often considered in selected high-risk patients given increased CNS relapse risk.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Represents a distinct entity from DLBCL and Burkitt through concurrent lineage-relevant translocations. WHO 2022 requires MYC + BCL2 (with or without BCL6) for HGBL with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements diagnosis. MYC + BCL6 without BCL2 is no longer included in this entity and is generally classified as DLBCL, NOS or HGBL, NOS depending on morphology. Aggressive biology reflects combined effects of MYC-driven proliferation and BCL2-mediated apoptosis evasion. Outcomes after CAR-T are still being defined and poor response is not established as a defining feature.

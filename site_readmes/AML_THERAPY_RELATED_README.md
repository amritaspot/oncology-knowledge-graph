# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AML_THERAPY_RELATED

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Adverse risk; often carries adverse cytogenetics (complex, monosomal karyotype, del(5q)/del(7q), TP53). Behaviour otherwise similar to AML NOS.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Poor prognosis is driven largely by adverse genomic features (TP53, complex, -5/-7) and patient/treatment context rather than treatment history alone. Treatment generally follows other AML risk/genotype-based approaches — e.g. venetoclax + azacitidine for unfit patients or intensive chemotherapy with consideration of allogeneic transplant in eligible patients.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Cytotoxic therapy can select clones with pre-existing mutations (including TP53-mutant clonal haematopoiesis) or contribute to specific fusion events (for example KMT2A rearrangements). Poor prognosis is driven largely by adverse genomic features rather than treatment history alone. Management is generally aligned with other adverse-risk AML, incorporating fitness and targetable alterations.

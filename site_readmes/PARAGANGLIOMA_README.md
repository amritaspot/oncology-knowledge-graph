# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PARAGANGLIOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Behaviour similar to pheochromocytoma. Higher metastatic risk in extra-adrenal sympathetic paragangliomas and SDHB-mutant tumours. Bone and lymph nodes are common metastatic sites, followed by liver and lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Head/neck PGL: parasympathetic origin, often non-secretory, SDHD/SDHB association, highly vascular. Sympathetic (thoracoabdominal) PGL: secretory, higher metastatic risk. SDHB carriers require lifelong surveillance.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same neural crest lineage as pheochromocytoma but extra-adrenal. Parasympathetic (H&N) paragangliomas are usually non-secretory but highly vascular; sympathetic (thoracoabdominal) are secretory. SDHB carriers require lifelong surveillance. Same SDHx-driven pseudohypoxia biology as seen in SDH-deficient neoplasia.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SMALL_BOWEL_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Not separately reported in Riihimäki 2018. Clinical: liver most common, followed by peritoneum; distant nodal and lung metastases also occur.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Predisposing conditions: Lynch syndrome, FAP, Peutz-Jeghers, celiac disease, Crohn disease. Late diagnosis due to non-specific symptoms.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Genomically distinct from colorectal adenocarcinoma despite morphologic similarity. Duodenal, ampullary, jejunal, and ileal subtypes have subtle biological differences. Late diagnosis reflects non-specific symptoms and low index of suspicion; ampullary tumours present earlier due to biliary obstruction.

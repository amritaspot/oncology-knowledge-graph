# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PRIMARY_CNS_LYMPHOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
DLBCL biology confined to the CNS (brain, leptomeninges, eyes/vitreoretina, and/or spinal cord). Systemic staging is required to exclude occult systemic lymphoma. Extraneural systemic metastasis at presentation is absent by definition and relapse is usually within the CNS/eye; see PRIMARY_CNS_LYMPHOMA_B_CELL under B_CELL_NHL for lymphoma framework.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
In immunocompetent patients, PCNSL is usually DLBCL confined to the CNS. Standard therapy requires high-dose methotrexate-based regimens that penetrate the CNS; rituximab is commonly used in many protocols despite limited CNS penetration. Autologous SCT consolidation is used in some protocols. Distinct from many immunocompromised (especially HIV-associated) PCNSL cases, which are often EBV-driven.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Cross-reference with PRIMARY_CNS_LYMPHOMA_B_CELL under B_CELL_NHL for shared lineage biology. MYD88 and CD79B co-mutation is enriched in PCNSL and has been associated with response to BTK inhibition in studies, but this is not a definitive standalone predictive biomarker in routine care. EBV-driven biology in immunocompromised hosts contrasts with most immunocompetent CNS-tropic DLBCL. Requires BBB-penetrant high-dose methotrexate-based therapy.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CUTANEOUS_MELANOMA

### metastasis_patterns.aggregation_note
Riihimäki 'Melanoma' category likely dominated by cutaneous melanoma. Acral, mucosal, and uveal melanoma have distinct organotropism (see separate entries).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Nervous system/brain; a leading metastatic site in population data, and melanoma has one of the highest brain-metastasis propensities among solid tumours.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.PULMONARY.notes
Lung.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.DERMATOLOGIC.notes
Skin/subcutaneous tissue (includes local-regional cutaneous/subcutaneous metastasis and distant skin involvement in registry-style data).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL_OTHER.notes
GI tract (small bowel classically overrepresented clinically/pathologically).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.ENDOCRINE.notes
Adrenal gland.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL.notes
Peritoneum.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TMB (UV-signature) and TIL density help explain the paradigm-changing IO responses. Desmoplastic melanoma has high IO response rates despite distinctive genomics and often fewer canonical BRAF/NRAS drivers. Nivolumab + relatlimab (RELATIVITY-047) established LAG-3 blockade in first-line.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
MITF-based cell state model explains phenotype switching between differentiated (MITF-high, drug-sensitive) and dedifferentiated (MITF-low, drug-resistant) states — mechanism of BRAF/MEK inhibitor resistance. Neural crest origin shared with schwannoma, MTC, pheochromocytoma, olfactory neuroblastoma. Desmoplastic variant has neural crest-like phenotype with schwannian features.

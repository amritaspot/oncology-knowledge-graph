# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MANTLE_CELL_LYMPHOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
t(11;14)/cyclin D1. Wide spectrum from indolent leukaemic non-nodal (often SOX11-negative, better prognosis) to aggressive blastoid/pleomorphic. MIPI prognostic score.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
SOX11-negative leukaemic non-nodal MCL is an indolent variant that may be amenable to watch-and-wait in selected patients. TP53-mutant MCL responds poorly to standard immunochemotherapy and often has inferior outcomes with covalent BTK inhibitors; CAR-T or clinical trial may be preferred in eligible patients. Pirtobrutinib (non-covalent BTK) is active after covalent BTK resistance.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
SOX11 expression helps distinguish typical (aggressive nodal) from leukaemic non-nodal (more indolent) MCL. t(11;14)/CCND1 is defining but not sufficient for disease. Blastoid/pleomorphic transformation reflects progression to a more aggressive phenotype. BTK inhibitors exploit BCR signalling dependency; non-covalent BTK inhibition can retain activity after C481-mediated resistance.

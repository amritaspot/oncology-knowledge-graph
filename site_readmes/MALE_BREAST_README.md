# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MALE_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
Not included in Riihimäki 2018 breast cohort (female-only). Male breast cancer is uncommon and metastatic site data are limited; available series suggest a pattern broadly similar to female hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, with bone a common metastatic site.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Older presentation, delayed diagnosis. HR+/HER2- luminal biology dominates. Strong BRCA2 predisposition.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Predominantly HR-positive luminal biology; lobular carcinoma is very rare. BRCA2 germline mutation is a major inherited predisposition and is found in about 10% of unselected male breast cancers in many series, with higher rates in selected familial cohorts. Same endocrine and targeted therapies are generally used as in female breast cancer. Klinefelter syndrome (XXY) also predisposes.

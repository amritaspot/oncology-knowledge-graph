# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: RHABDOMYOSARCOMA_EMBRYONAL

### metastasis_patterns_note
Better prognosis than alveolar. Metastasis most commonly to lung; bone marrow and bone can also occur.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Better prognosis than alveolar. RAS pathway alterations are common in fusion-negative/embryonal RMS. Botryoid variant in hollow organs is a favourable subtype. Sclerosing/spindle cell MYOD1 L122R is distinct aggressive variant.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
No FOXO1 fusion (unlike fusion-positive ARMS). Better prognosis than fusion-positive ARMS. Botryoid variant in hollow organs (bladder, vagina, bile duct) has best prognosis. Spindle cell / sclerosing MYOD1 L122R variant is distinct aggressive entity.

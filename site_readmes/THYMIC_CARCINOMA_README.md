# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: THYMIC_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive tumour with high metastatic potential. Reported in review literature: many patients present with locally advanced disease involving contiguous mediastinal structures; distant metastatic spread is common, including pleura/pericardium, lymph nodes, lung, liver, bone, and less commonly other organs. Exact per-organ population frequencies are not well standardised across series and were not harvested in this pass.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Immune checkpoint inhibitors show antitumour activity in a subset of recurrent/metastatic thymic carcinomas, but clinically significant immune-related adverse events can still occur. Aggressive behaviour with early metastasis.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Loss of organotypic thymic differentiation helps explain the much lower frequency of autoimmune paraneoplastic syndromes than in thymoma, though they are not entirely absent. Immune checkpoint inhibition is more established in thymic carcinoma than in thymoma, but toxicity monitoring remains essential. Aggressive biology with earlier metastasis than thymoma.

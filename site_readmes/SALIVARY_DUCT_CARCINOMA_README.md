# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SALIVARY_DUCT_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive salivary gland carcinoma with frequent regional nodal involvement and a high rate of distant metastasis; lung and bone are the most common distant sites, with liver also reported in case series.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
AR-driven biology parallels apocrine/luminal programs and many tumors appear relatively immune-cold. HER2-positive subset may benefit from HER2 blockade. Aggressive with regional and distant metastasis.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
AR-positive luminal/apocrine biology supports use of androgen deprivation therapy in selected recurrent/metastatic cases. HER2-amplified/overexpressed subset (~25-40%) supports trastuzumab-based therapy and has shown activity with trastuzumab deruxtecan. Molecular features overlap with apocrine breast carcinoma, including frequent TP53 and PI3K-pathway alterations, and recurrent HRAS mutations in a subset.

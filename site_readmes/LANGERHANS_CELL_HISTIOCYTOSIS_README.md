# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LANGERHANS_CELL_HISTIOCYTOSIS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
BRAF V600E mutation in ~50-60%; MAPK pathway activation is near-universal. Ranges from single-system unifocal (excellent prognosis) to multi-system with risk-organ involvement (worse prognosis). BRAF/MEK inhibitors are active.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
MAPK pathway activation is near-universal and underlies efficacy of BRAF/MEK inhibitors. Cobimetinib has regulatory approval for certain histiocytic neoplasms. Risk-organ (liver, spleen, marrow) involvement or CNS-LCH requires intensive systemic therapy.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
MAPK pathway activation is near-universal across LCH. BRAF V600E is the most common driver (~50-60%). Cobimetinib has regulatory approval in histiocytic neoplasms in some jurisdictions/settings. Risk-organ involvement (LCH-RO+  liver, spleen, marrow) reflects higher-risk disease. Adult pulmonary LCH is smoker-associated.

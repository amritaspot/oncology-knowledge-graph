# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ADRENOCORTICAL_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
diSibio 2008 adrenal row (n=6 primaries, 31 total lesions) too small for reliable rates. ACC clinical pattern from ESE/ENSAT guidelines and large retrospective series: lung is the most common distant site, followed by liver; bone and distant lymph nodes/peritoneum are also recognized metastatic sites. Local extension into renal vein/IVC and kidney is also common. Precise percentages vary by series and are not reliably standardized.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Cortisol excess drives immunosuppression and may contribute to limited immunotherapy responsiveness. Mitotane (adrenolytic) remains cornerstone systemic therapy despite modest efficacy. Molecular subtyping (C1A/C1B in transcriptomic studies; other classifier schemes also reported) is emerging.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Adrenal cortex is embryologically and lineage-distinct from adrenal medulla (mesoderm vs neural crest respectively). Steroidogenic capacity produces clinical syndromes (Cushing's from cortisol, virilisation from androgens, feminisation from oestrogens, rarely primary aldosteronism). SF-1 is a lineage-defining nuclear marker. Cortisol excess itself contributes to an immunosuppressive TME.

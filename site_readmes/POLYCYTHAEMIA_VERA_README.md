# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: POLYCYTHAEMIA_VERA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
JAK2 mutation in ~98% overall (V617F in ~95%, exon 12 in most of the remainder). Treatment: phlebotomy to Hct <45%, low-dose aspirin, cytoreduction (hydroxyurea, interferon alfa including ropeginterferon alfa-2b, ruxolitinib) in high-risk or selected patients.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
JAK2 mutation drives disease in nearly all cases. Phlebotomy to Hct <45% + aspirin is foundation; hydroxyurea or interferon (including ropeginterferon alfa-2b) for high-risk or selected patients. Ruxolitinib for hydroxyurea-resistant/intolerant disease. Thrombosis is a major complication; Budd-Chiari is a classic association.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
HSC-origin JAK2 mutation explains multilineage proliferation. JAK2 V617F drives constitutive JAK-STAT signalling downstream of EPO receptor and other cytokine receptors — physiologically regulated erythropoiesis becomes constitutive. Post-PV myelofibrosis and AML transformation represent clonal evolution.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: OVARIAN_HIGH_GRADE_SEROUS

### metastasis_patterns.aggregation_note
Riihimäki source combines all ovarian subtypes. HGSC is the dominant contributor (~70% of epithelial ovarian cancer) so aggregate data broadly reflects HGSC behaviour, especially transcoelomic peritoneal spread. Non-serous subtypes may differ, and contralateral ovary is more often synchronous pelvic extension than true distant metastasis.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL.notes
Peritoneum; dominant pattern — transcoelomic spread across peritoneal surfaces.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver (often capsular / surface rather than parenchymal).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.PULMONARY_PLEURA.notes
Pleura; often malignant effusion.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.PULMONARY.notes
Lung parenchyma.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL_OTHER.notes
Other GI (bowel serosa involvement).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Nervous system; uncommon.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; uncommon.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.REPRODUCTIVE_GONADAL.notes
Contralateral ovary; often represents synchronous pelvic/adnexal involvement rather than true distant metastasis.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
IO monotherapy has limited benefit; combinations (with PARP or bevacizumab) are the direction. Tubal fimbria origin (STIC lesions) increasingly recognised.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Fallopian tube fimbria origin has transformed understanding — risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy in BRCA carriers now emphasises tubal removal. Near-universal TP53 mutation and HRD (BRCA-associated ~20-25% overall including germline/somatic, with additional non-BRCA HRR defects) drive PARP inhibitor sensitivity. STIC lesions are recognisable precursor. WT1+/PAX8+ Müllerian phenotype distinguishes from other pelvic malignancies.

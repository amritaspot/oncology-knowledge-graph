# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CML

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
TKI-transformed disease (imatinib and successors). Blast crisis is uncommon in TKI-treated patients but historically was fatal. Extramedullary blast crisis can present at any anatomic site.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TKI-transformed disease with treatment-free remission achievable in sustained deep molecular response. Asciminib (STAMP allosteric inhibitor) offers a new mechanism for resistant/intolerant patients. Blast crisis rare in TKI-treated patients but historically fatal.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
BCR-ABL1 fusion (t(9;22), Philadelphia chromosome) in HSC drives disease — same fusion in B-cell progenitor causes Ph+ B-ALL (different cell of origin, different clinical phenotype). Chronic phase reflects retained granulocytic differentiation; blast crisis represents dedifferentiation with either myeloid or lymphoid phenotype (reflecting HSC lineage potential). TKI-transformed with treatment-free remission achievable.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: COLON_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among colon cancer patients with recorded metastasis (any stage; ~30% of the total 31,285 colon cancer cohort in the source study). Numerator = patients with metastasis to that organ; denominator = all patients with any recorded metastatic site.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver; most common metastatic site.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.PULMONARY.notes
Thoracic organs (lung + pleura + other respiratory).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.GASTROINTESTINAL.notes
Peritoneum; source lists peritoneal metastasis under gastrointestinal spread. Elevated in mucinous / signet-ring subtypes (OR 3.80 vs generic adenocarcinoma).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Nervous system; higher when co-occurring with pulmonary metastasis.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; uncommon overall but reported at approximately 6% among colon cancer patients with recorded metastasis in the registry study. Also disproportionately seen as solitary metastasis in early-stage patients.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.REPRODUCTIVE_GONADAL.notes
Ovarian metastases occur, especially in women with colon cancer (more common than in rectal), often together with peritoneal spread. Frequency not enumerated separately.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Right-sided CRC enriched for MSI-H, BRAF-mutant, CMS1/3 — worse survival but IO responsive if MSI-H. Left-sided predominantly CMS2 (canonical) — anti-EGFR benefit. CMS4 (mesenchymal, worst prognosis) resists IO.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
CMS classification (CMS1 MSI immune, CMS2 canonical, CMS3 metabolic, CMS4 mesenchymal) reflects distinct lineage/TME states with therapeutic implications. Right- vs left-sided differences reflect embryologic origin (midgut vs hindgut) and distinct precursor lesion biology.

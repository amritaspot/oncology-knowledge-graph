# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: METAPLASTIC_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive breast cancer subtype, usually triple-negative; compared with conventional invasive ductal carcinoma, it shows lower axillary nodal involvement and a tendency toward hematogenous spread, especially to lung. Brain metastases can occur, but a consistently higher rate than typical TNBC is not firmly established.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Aggressive breast cancer subtype with worse prognosis than conventional TNBC in many series and a higher rate of chemotherapy resistance. PI3K/AKT pathway alterations are enriched, particularly involving PIK3CA and PTEN.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Marked EMT/plasticity contributes to distinct biology and frequent chemoresistance. Recurrent genomic alterations include TP53 and PI3K pathway abnormalities (for example PIK3CA mutation and PTEN loss); no pathognomonic driver fusion is established for conventional metaplastic breast carcinoma. Sarcomatoid/spindle cell variants may be misdiagnosed as primary breast sarcoma without epithelial marker confirmation.

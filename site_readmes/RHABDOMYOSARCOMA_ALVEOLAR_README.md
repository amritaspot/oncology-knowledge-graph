# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: RHABDOMYOSARCOMA_ALVEOLAR

### metastasis_patterns_note
Paediatric/AYA. Distinctive bone marrow involvement (reported in ~20% of patients with metastatic disease at diagnosis; marrow involvement can mimic leukaemia). Lung, bone, and lymph node are also common metastatic sites. PAX3/7-FOXO1 fusion.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Fusion-positive ARMS (PAX3/7-FOXO1) has worse prognosis than fusion-negative. Fusion-negative ARMS behaves like ERMS. Bone marrow disease at diagnosis, particularly in metastatic cases, can mimic leukaemia. Vincristine-actinomycin-cyclophosphamide (VAC) backbone.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Fusion-locking of PAX3/7-FOXO1 keeps cells in an early myoblast state  prevents terminal differentiation into myotubes. Fusion presence defines biology more than histology  fusion-positive ARMS is a distinct entity with worse prognosis; fusion-negative ARMS behaves like ERMS. Bone marrow disease, especially in metastatic presentations, can mimic leukaemia.

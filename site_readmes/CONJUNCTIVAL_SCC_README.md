# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CONJUNCTIVAL_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Usually locally aggressive; regional lymph node metastasis is uncommon, and distant metastasis is rare; when it occurs, spread is typically to preauricular/submandibular/cervical nodes and occasionally lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Also called ocular surface squamous neoplasia (OSSN). More common and aggressive in HIV+ populations, especially sub-Saharan Africa. Topical interferon-b1, 5-FU, mitomycin-C effective for CIN and limited-invasive disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same limbal stem cell biology of ocular surface as UV-related conditions of ocular surface. Also called ocular surface squamous neoplasia (OSSN). HIV+ populations (especially sub-Saharan Africa) have markedly higher incidence and can show more aggressive behaviour. Topical chemotherapy (interferon-b1, 5-FU, MMC) is effective for early disease.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: BURKITT_LYMPHOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
MYC translocation, extremely high proliferation index (Ki-67 near 100%). Tumour lysis syndrome risk at initiation of therapy. Curable with intensive regimens (CODOX-M/IVAC, DA-EPOCH-R, hyper-CVAD).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Curable with intensive regimens (CODOX-M/IVAC, DA-EPOCH-R, hyper-CVAD). Very high tumour lysis syndrome risk at initiation — rasburicase and aggressive hydration/monitoring mandatory. CNS prophylaxis/intrathecal-directed therapy is incorporated into intensive regimens because of high CNS risk. BCL2-negative or weak helps distinguish from other aggressive B-cell lymphomas.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
MYC translocation is defining — drives extremely high proliferation and distinctive metabolism. Three clinical variants (endemic African, sporadic Western, immunodeficiency-associated) share molecular biology but differ in EBV co-involvement (endemic near-universal, sporadic ~15-30%, immunodeficiency-associated ~25-40%). BCL2 is usually negative or weak, helping distinguish from some other aggressive B-cell lymphomas. Extreme proliferation drives dramatic TLS risk at induction.

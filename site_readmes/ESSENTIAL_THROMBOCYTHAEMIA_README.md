# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ESSENTIAL_THROMBOCYTHAEMIA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
JAK2, CALR, MPL mutations. Risk-stratified by IPSET-thrombosis (age, prior thrombosis, JAK2 status, cardiovascular risk factors in the original model).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinguishing ET from prefibrotic PMF is critical — different prognosis and treatment. JAK2-mutant ET has higher thrombosis risk than CALR-mutant. Aspirin + cytoreduction (hydroxyurea, anagrelide, interferon) risk-based. Acquired vWF deficiency at very high platelet counts causes paradoxical bleeding.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Megakaryocytic lineage bias distinguishes ET from PV (trilineage) despite shared JAK-STAT activation biology. CALR mutations produce mutant calreticulin that binds and activates MPL — mechanistically converging on JAK-STAT activation.

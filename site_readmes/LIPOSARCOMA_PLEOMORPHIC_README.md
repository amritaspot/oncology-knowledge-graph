# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LIPOSARCOMA_PLEOMORPHIC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive; lung-dominant metastasis like most high-grade pleomorphic soft tissue sarcomas.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rarest liposarcoma subtype. Distinguished from UPS with lipoblastic/metaplastic change by identification of genuine pleomorphic lipoblasts. Aggressive with predominantly hematogenous metastasis, especially to lung, like other high-grade pleomorphic sarcomas.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Rarest liposarcoma subtype. Distinguishing from UPS with metaplastic lipoblasts is critical — requires identification of true pleomorphic lipoblasts. Lacks the MDM2 amplicon of WD/DD LPS and the DDIT3 fusions of myxoid LPS. Complex karyotype rather than defining single alteration. Aggressive with predominantly lung metastasis pattern like other high-grade pleomorphic sarcomas.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MUCOSAL_MELANOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinct from cutaneous melanoma in behavior; aggressive with high locoregional recurrence. Distant metastases commonly involve lung, liver, bone, and brain depending on primary site.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Aggressive with early metastasis. KIT and SF3B1 enriched. Anatomic constraints often limit surgery. Combination nivolumab + ipilimumab has shown higher activity than single-agent PD-1 therapy, but treatment selection depends on stage, resectability, comorbidity, and guidelines.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct anatomic site biology from cutaneous  mucosal melanocytes at sinonasal/oral/anorectal/genitourinary sites have distinct molecular landscape. Low TMB is one contributor to generally less robust immunotherapy responses relative to cutaneous melanoma. Combination nivolumab + ipilimumab may have greater activity than monotherapy in advanced disease.

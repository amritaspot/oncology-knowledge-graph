# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: CLL_SLL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Autoimmune complications common: AIHA, ITP, PRCA. Hypogammaglobulinaemia. Richter's transformation to DLBCL (~2-10%) or Hodgkin lymphoma (rare) — biopsy any rapidly enlarging node, discordant PET SUV, or dramatic clinical change.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Modern therapy is targeted (BTK inhibitors, venetoclax-based regimens including venetoclax + obinutuzumab). Autoimmune cytopenias, hypogammaglobulinaemia, and Richter transformation to DLBCL are complications. Fixed-duration venetoclax combinations can achieve uMRD in many patients (e.g. CLL14, MURANO).

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
IGHV mutation status is prognostic and reflects cell-of-origin biology — mutated has better prognosis than unmutated. Same lineage biology across CLL (marrow/blood presentation) and SLL (nodal presentation). Autoimmune cytopenias reflect immune dysregulation associated with the disease. Richter transformation to DLBCL represents clonal progression.

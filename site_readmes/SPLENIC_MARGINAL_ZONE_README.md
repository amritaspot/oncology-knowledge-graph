# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SPLENIC_MARGINAL_ZONE

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Indolent. HCV association in some cohorts. Splenectomy or rituximab-based therapy.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Villous lymphocytes on blood film. Massive splenomegaly typical. HCV eradication may induce lymphoma remission (Hermine et al. NEJM 2002) — antiviral first-line if HCV+. Splenectomy historically curative; rituximab-based therapy now preferred non-surgical option.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Villous lymphocytes on blood film reflect distinctive marginal zone B-cell morphology. Splenic tropism reflects lineage biology. HCV association (in some geographic regions) suggests antigen-driven proliferation — HCV eradication can induce remission (Hermine NEJM 2002). Same NOTCH2-driven biology as other MZL types.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NODAL_MARGINAL_ZONE

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Indolent; less common than extranodal MZL and splenic MZL. Similar management to other indolent B-cell lymphomas.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Nodal MZL is the least common major MZL subtype. Management often parallels other indolent B-cell lymphomas (observation in selected patients; anti-CD20-based therapy when treatment is needed). NOTCH2 mutations are recurrent across MZL subtypes but are not unique to nodal MZL.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Least common MZL subtype but shares broad lineage biology with splenic and extranodal MZL. NOTCH2 signaling is important in marginal zone B-cell differentiation, and recurrent NOTCH2 mutations are described in MZL, though they are not specific and do not define all cases. Treatment principles are those of an indolent B-cell lymphoma rather than a site-specific targeted-driver disease.

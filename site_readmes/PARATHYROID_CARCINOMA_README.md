# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PARATHYROID_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Rare; local invasion dominates; distant metastasis usually occurs late, most often to lung, bone, and liver. Death often results from refractory hypercalcaemia rather than metastatic burden.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Death typically from hypercalcaemia rather than metastatic burden. Cinacalcet (calcimimetic) is used for medical management of hypercalcaemia; denosumab or bisphosphonates may also be used for hypercalcaemia control and skeletal protection.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Very rare cancer in which death often results from functional hormone excess (hypercalcaemia) rather than metastatic burden. Hyperparathyroidism-jaw tumour syndrome (germline CDC73) predisposes. Distinct from parathyroid adenoma by unequivocal invasion or metastasis criteria.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: DLBCL_ABC

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Historically worse prognosis than GCB with R-CHOP. Constitutive NF-baB pathway activation is characteristic; CD79B and MYD88 mutations are enriched. Some molecular subsets, particularly MCD/C5, may benefit from novel agents such as BTK inhibitors in selected settings.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Historically worse prognosis than GCB with R-CHOP, though the gap may narrow with modern therapy. LymphGen MCD cluster (often MYD88+CD79B) has shown signals of benefit from ibrutinib-containing approaches in subgroup analyses, but this is not definitive standard-of-care. CNS-directed therapy is considered based on CNS-IPI and specific high-risk extranodal/genetic features rather than ABC COO alone.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
ABC subtype comprises roughly one-third of DLBCL by gene-expression profiling. MYD88 and CD79B alterations are enriched and converge on NF-baB pathway activation. Plasmablastic features may be seen in a subset. Same B-cell antigen expression enables CAR-T and bispecific therapy.

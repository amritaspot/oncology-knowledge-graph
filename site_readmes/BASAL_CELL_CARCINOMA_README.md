# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: BASAL_CELL_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Extremely low metastatic rate (estimated <0.1%); locally destructive. When it occurs, spread most often to regional lymph nodes, lung, and bone.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Extremely low metastatic rate but locally destructive. Hedgehog pathway alterations are near-universal — vismodegib and sonidegib are effective. Cemiplimab is now available for Hedgehog-inhibitor-refractory disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Hedgehog pathway alterations are near-universal — vismodegib and sonidegib target SMO. Basal cell nevus (Gorlin) syndrome from germline PTCH1 loss predisposes to multiple BCCs plus medulloblastoma (same Hedgehog biology). Locally destructive but very rarely metastasises — reflects lineage biology of proliferation with impaired invasion competence.

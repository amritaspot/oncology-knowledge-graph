# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MESOTHELIOMA_PLEURAL

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among 164 MPM patients referred to a Phase I trials unit — a selected advanced-disease cohort, likely higher metastatic rates than the general MPM population. 67% had distant metastatic disease at referral. MPM is traditionally characterised by local pleural spread; distant metastasis is more common than historically recognised.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.PULMONARY.notes
Contralateral lung; most common distant site.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.GASTROINTESTINAL.notes
Peritoneum; more likely in epithelioid than biphasic/sarcomatoid subtypes (p=0.015).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY_AND_OTHER_VISCERAL.notes
Visceral metastases (including liver and other abdominal viscera, grouped in source); older autopsy series report liver ~30%, nodal ~40%.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
IO benefit is greatest in sarcomatoid/biphasic in CheckMate 743. BAP1 loss defines a hereditary mesothelioma predisposition syndrome (also RCC, uveal melanoma).

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Calretinin and WT1 are mesothelial lineage markers. BAP1 germline (hereditary mesothelioma) predisposes to same neural crest / mesothelial cell lineage tumours: mesothelioma, uveal melanoma, RCC. Asbestos is dominant carcinogen with decades-long latency.

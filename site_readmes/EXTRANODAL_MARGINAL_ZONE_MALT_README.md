# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: EXTRANODAL_MARGINAL_ZONE_MALT

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Indolent; chronic antigenic stimulation model. Site-specific treatment considerations (antibiotic therapy for infection-driven forms). Transformation to DLBCL occurs in a minority of cases, typically estimated at <10%.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
H. pylori eradication is first-line for gastric MALT — induces remission in most H. pylori+ localized disease. t(11;18) predicts H. pylori eradication failure. Site-specific antigenic drivers determine treatment approach. Transformation to DLBCL possible.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same marginal zone B-cell lineage as other MZL types but with distinctive mucosal antigen-driven biology. Antigen removal (H. pylori eradication) can induce remission — paradigmatic example of antigen dependence. t(11;18) BIRC3-MALT1 confers relative antigen independence and predicts eradication failure. Site-specific antigenic drivers determine treatment approach.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Strong association with alcohol, tobacco, and (in high-incidence regions) hot beverages / nitrosamine exposure. Immune checkpoint inhibitor responses are clinically meaningful in biomarker-selected and guideline-defined settings; adjuvant nivolumab improves outcomes after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy and surgery with residual disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same p63/SOX2 lineage circuit as other squamous cancers. Distinct from esophageal adenocarcinoma in both cell of origin (squamous vs metaplastic glandular) and geography (SCC dominant in East Asian, African populations; adenocarcinoma in Western). Lugol iodine chromoendoscopy identifies subclinical lesions via loss of glycogen.

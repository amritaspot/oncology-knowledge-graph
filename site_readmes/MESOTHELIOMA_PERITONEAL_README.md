# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MESOTHELIOMA_PERITONEAL

### metastasis_patterns_note
Clinical behaviour: predominantly locoregional peritoneal spread; nodal and distant/extraperitoneal metastases are uncommon compared with pleural mesothelioma, especially in epithelioid disease.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Asbestos association is generally weaker than in pleural mesothelioma. CRS + HIPEC has changed outcomes for selected patients, especially epithelioid variants. Well-differentiated papillary mesothelial tumor and multicystic mesothelial proliferation are distinct low-grade/borderline mesothelial entities.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same mesothelial lineage as pleural mesothelioma but distinct anatomic and clinical behaviour. Well-differentiated papillary mesothelial tumor and multicystic mesothelial proliferation of the peritoneum are low-grade/borderline entities with better prognosis. CRS + HIPEC changed outcomes for selected patients with peritoneal-confined disease, especially epithelioid variants.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ESOPHAGEAL_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
See ESOPHAGEAL_SCC — Riihimäki 2018 aggregates SCC and adenocarcinoma. Wu et al. 2017 SEER analysis (n=1,470, PMID: 28744156) reports liver 32%, distant lymph nodes 28%, lung 21%, bone 15%, brain 3.4% across esophageal cancer overall — not separately extracted for adenocarcinoma vs SCC in this pass.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rising incidence in Western populations linked to GERD, obesity, Barrett's. GEJ tumours share biology and are staged with esophageal or gastric depending on epicentre.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Represents lineage switch from native squamous to glandular/intestinalized epithelium driven by chronic reflux injury. Barrett's is the canonical precursor lesion. Molecularly aligns more closely with chromosomal-instability upper GI adenocarcinoma than with ESCC. Rising in Western populations with obesity/GERD epidemic.

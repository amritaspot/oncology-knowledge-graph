# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MEDULLOBLASTOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Leptomeningeal / CSF spread is common ('drop metastases'); craniospinal MRI + CSF cytology essential for staging. Extraneural metastasis is rare but reported, most often involving bone, bone marrow, and lymph nodes.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Molecular subgroup (WNT/SHH/Group 3/Group 4) drives modern management. SMO inhibitors for selected SHH-pathway-activated tumors. Craniospinal RT + chemo standard. WNT subgroup is the target of de-escalation trials.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct cells of origin for each subgroup explain distinct molecular biology and clinical behaviour. Modern management is subgroup-directed: WNT for de-escalation trials (excellent prognosis), SHH for selected SMO inhibitors (vismodegib, sonidegib), Group 3 for intensive therapy (worst prognosis), Group 4 with subgroup-appropriate treatment.

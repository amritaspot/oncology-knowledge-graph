# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PINEAL_PARENCHYMAL

### metastasis_patterns_note
Pineoblastoma commonly disseminates via CSF/leptomeningeal pathways; staging typically includes MRI brain and spine and CSF cytology. Pineocytoma is indolent.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Pineoblastoma is an embryonal pineal parenchymal tumour with propensity for CSF/leptomeningeal dissemination. Trilateral retinoblastoma (bilateral retinoblastoma + pineoblastoma) occurs in some germline RB1 carriers. DICER1 syndrome association.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
RB1-altered pineoblastoma is linked to trilateral retinoblastoma syndrome in germline RB1 carriers. DICER1 syndrome predisposes to pineoblastoma (as well as pleuropulmonary blastoma, Sertoli-Leydig cell tumour, and other neoplasms). Because of CSF dissemination risk, staging includes neuroaxis evaluation; craniospinal irradiation may be used in appropriate pineoblastoma treatment protocols.

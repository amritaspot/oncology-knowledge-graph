# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ATRT

### metastasis_patterns_note
Paediatric; SMARCB1/SMARCA4-driven. Leptomeningeal/CSF dissemination is common and may be present at diagnosis. Extraneural metastases are uncommon but have been reported.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Paediatric (most commonly in children <3 years, but can occur at other ages). SMARCB1 (INI-1) loss defines most cases. EZH2 inhibition with tazemetostat has biologic rationale in SMARCB1-deficient tumours, but ATRT management remains centered on multimodality therapy including surgery, chemotherapy, and focal craniospinal-directed radiotherapy as age-appropriate; high-dose chemotherapy with stem cell rescue is used in some protocols.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
SMARCB1/INI-1 loss is defining in most cases and parallels other SMARCB1-deficient tumours across sites. This chromatin remodelling defect is thought to contribute to the multi-lineage marker expression pattern. EZH2 inhibitors such as tazemetostat are under investigation/biologically relevant in SMARCB1-deficient tumours.

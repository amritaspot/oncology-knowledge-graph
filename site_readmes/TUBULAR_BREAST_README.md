# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: TUBULAR_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
HR+ low-grade; excellent prognosis; axillary nodal metastases can occur but distant metastasis is rare/uncommon.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Excellent prognosis. Chemotherapy typically not required if small and node-negative. Endocrine therapy standard.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Excellent prognosis and endocrine-responsive biology reflect preserved luminal differentiation. Chemotherapy typically not required if small and node-negative. Endocrine therapy standard. Distinguishing from adenosis and complex sclerosing lesions requires careful pathology.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MENINGIOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Most are WHO grade 1 and benign. Grade 2/3 can recur locally; extracranial metastasis is very rare (<1%) and is reported most often in WHO grade 3 (anaplastic) meningioma, with lung/pleura the most common site, followed by liver and bone.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
SSTR2 expression enables DOTATATE PET imaging and PRRT (theranostic strategy). Grade 3 (anaplastic) is where extracranial metastasis is most often seen, though still rare. Molecular criteria increasingly override morphologic grading.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Neural crest / mesodermal dual origin distinguishes cranial from spinal meningeal biology. SSTR2 expression is lineage-defining and enables DOTATATE PET and PRRT strategy. Progesterone receptor expression is characteristic (may help explain hormone responsiveness in some tumors). WHO CNS5 integrates molecular criteria (CDKN2A/B, TERT) with morphology for grading; H3K27me3 loss is prognostic but is not itself a formal grading criterion.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PANCREATIC_NET_G2

### metastasis_patterns_note
See PANCREATIC_NET_G1; higher metastatic potential than G1. Common metastatic sites include liver (most common), regional/distant lymph nodes, and less commonly bone and peritoneum.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Intermediate biology and prognosis. Same SSTR-driven strategies may apply when receptor expression is retained.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same islet cell lineage as G1 with higher proliferation. SSTR2 expression is often retained, supporting somatostatin receptor imaging and potential PRRT eligibility. Common molecular alterations overlap with G1, including MEN1, DAXX, ATRX, and mTOR-pathway gene alterations; recurrent driver fusions are not a hallmark of well-differentiated pancreatic NETs.

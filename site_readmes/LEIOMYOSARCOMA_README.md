# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LEIOMYOSARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Sarcoma; lung-dominant metastasis (~50-70%), then liver, bone. Uterine LMS also spreads to peritoneum/abdomen.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Anatomic sites: retroperitoneal, uterine (see UTERINE_LEIOMYOSARCOMA), soft tissue extremity, cutaneous, vascular (IVC leiomyosarcoma). Doxorubicin ± dacarbazine, or gemcitabine + docetaxel are standard. Trabectedin, pazopanib in later lines. Leiomyosarcoma is characterized by a complex karyotype and lacks a recurrent pathognomonic driver fusion.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Uterine LMS is a distinct entity (see UTERINE_LEIOMYOSARCOMA) despite shared smooth muscle lineage. Site-specific biology explains why anatomic site matters therapeutically. TP53 mutations common. Complex karyotype rather than defining single alteration or recurrent driver fusion. Immune-cold typically; poor IO responses.

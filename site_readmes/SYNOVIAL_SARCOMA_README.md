# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SYNOVIAL_SARCOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Lung-dominant (approximately 70–80% of distant metastases); can also spread to bone and, less commonly, lymph nodes. SS18-SSX fusion.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Afamitresgene autoleucel (afami-cel, TCR-T targeting MAGE-A4) is approved for advanced synovial sarcoma in HLA-A*02-eligible, antigen-positive patients. It is the first engineered cell therapy approved for a solid tumour. Young adult patients typical.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Despite the name, DOES NOT arise from synovium — historical misnomer. SS18-SSX fusion drives chromatin remodelling defect that produces the distinctive bipotent phenotype. Cancer-testis antigen expression has enabled TCR-T therapy; young adult patients typical (median age often in the 30s).

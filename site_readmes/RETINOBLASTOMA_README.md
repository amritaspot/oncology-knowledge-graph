# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: RETINOBLASTOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Paediatric tumour, RB1-driven in the vast majority of cases. Spread occurs by direct extension along the optic nerve with potential CNS/leptomeningeal involvement, extraocular/orbital extension, and haematogenous metastasis most often to bone marrow and bone. Trilateral retinoblastoma classically refers to heritable retinoblastoma with an intracranial midline primitive neuroectodermal tumour, most commonly pineoblastoma.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Bilateral / familial disease is usually due to germline RB1 alteration. Germline carriers have second cancer risk (osteosarcoma, soft tissue sarcoma, melanoma, brain tumours). Trilateral retinoblastoma classically refers to heritable retinoblastoma with an intracranial midline tumour, most commonly pineoblastoma. Modern management preserves eye when possible.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: FOLLICULAR_GRADE_1_2

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Indolent; t(14;18)/BCL2 hallmark. FLIPI, FLIPI2, PRIMA-PI prognostic scores. Watch-and-wait strategy for asymptomatic disease; treat when GELF/BNLI criteria met. Transformation to DLBCL ~2-3%/year.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
t(14;18)/BCL2 detectable in healthy people at low levels — clinical context essential. POD24 (progression <24 months after start of first-line chemoimmunotherapy) is very adverse and may prompt consideration of cellular therapy in appropriate patients. Mosunetuzumab and tazemetostat (EZH2 inhibitor) expand options; epcoritamab has emerging activity in FL.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Recapitulates germinal centre B-cell biology — CREBBP, KMT2D, EZH2, TNFRSF14 and EP300 are among the recurrently mutated genes disrupting normal germinal centre chromatin and immune dynamics. t(14;18)/BCL2 detectable in healthy people at low levels — clinical context essential. Transformation to DLBCL is a major clinical event. Tazemetostat (EZH2 inhibitor) exploits mutant EZH2 in a subset.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Very poor prognosis. Often presents with perforation, obstruction, or refractory celiac symptoms. MEITL is a distinct entity — not celiac-associated, more common in East Asia. Multimodal therapy (chemo + surgery) with poor outcomes.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Classic EATL and MEITL represent two distinct intraepithelial lymphocyte-derived entities. EATL emerges from long-standing celiac disease and refractory celiac disease type II. MEITL is not celiac-associated and more common in East Asia. Very poor prognosis for both. Intraepithelial lymphocyte lineage explains distinctive intestinal presentation.

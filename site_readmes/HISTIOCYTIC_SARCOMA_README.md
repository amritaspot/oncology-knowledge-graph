# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: HISTIOCYTIC_SARCOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Rare, aggressive malignancy of mature tissue histiocytes. Often presents at extranodal sites and may be localized or disseminated at diagnosis. Poor prognosis. Can arise from transdifferentiation of lymphoid neoplasms (e.g. follicular lymphoma, CLL/SLL).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rare aggressive malignancy of mature tissue histiocytes. May be localized or disseminated at diagnosis. Poor prognosis. Transdifferentiation from lymphoid neoplasm (same clone as prior FL/CLL) is documented. Cobimetinib has tumor-agnostic approval for histiocytic neoplasms in some jurisdictions; MAPK-directed therapy considered when pathway alterations are present.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Transdifferentiation from lymphoid neoplasm (documented by shared clonal rearrangements) represents extreme lineage plasticity — lymphoid clone acquires histiocytic phenotype. MAPK-driven biology in a subset enables targeted therapy. May be localized or disseminated at diagnosis; poor prognosis.

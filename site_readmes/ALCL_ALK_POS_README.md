### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Typically affects children and young adults. Prognosis is generally better than ALK-negative ALCL. Brentuximab vedotin + CHP is an established option in systemic ALCL, but outcomes are not uniformly excellent in every ALK-positive case. ALK inhibitors provide effective salvage. Nuclear+cytoplasmic ALK IHC pattern indicates NPM1-ALK fusion; cytoplasmic-only staining favors non-NPM1 partners.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
ALK fusion is the defining lineage-relevant event. ALK protein is not normally expressed in mature lymphocytes; aberrant expression drives characteristic biology. Typically affects children and young adults and has a more favorable prognosis than ALK-negative ALCL. ALK inhibitors, especially crizotinib, are effective salvage therapy; activity of later-generation ALK inhibitors has also been reported. Same core ALK-driven oncogenic principle is seen across other ALK-rearranged tumors.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PITUITARY_ADENOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Usually benign by histology. Reclassified as 'pituitary neuroendocrine tumour' (PitNET) in WHO 2022. True pituitary carcinoma is rare (<0.5%)  defined by craniospinal or systemic metastasis.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Hormonal function drives management more than morphology. Cabergoline for prolactinoma. Somatostatin analogues + GH-receptor antagonist for acromegaly. Ki-67 >3% and extensive p53 staining may raise concern for aggressive behaviour, but are not by themselves diagnostic of carcinoma or a formal WHO grade.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Modern pituitary tumour classification is lineage-based using transcription factor IHC (Pit-1, T-Pit, SF-1)  this reflects cell of origin rather than just hormone production. GNAS mutations are common in somatotroph tumours (and somatic GNAS activation also underlies McCune-Albright syndrome). USP8 mutations occur in a subset of corticotroph tumours. MEN1 syndrome predisposes to pituitary tumours.

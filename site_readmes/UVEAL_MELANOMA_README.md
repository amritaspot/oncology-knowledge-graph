# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: UVEAL_MELANOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Biologically distinct from cutaneous melanoma. Highly characteristic hepatotropic pattern: liver metastasis is the dominant first and overall metastatic site and is involved in ~80-90% of patients with metastatic uveal melanoma (often cited as ~90%; Kujala et al. 2003 IOVS and subsequent series). Median time from primary diagnosis to metastasis is commonly ~2-5 years, with substantial variability. Bone, lung, and skin/soft tissue are much less common than liver. GNAQ/GNA11 driver mutations are characteristic; BAP1 loss predicts metastasis.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinct biology from cutaneous — GNAQ/GNA11 drivers; BAP1 loss predicts metastasis. Hepatotropic metastasis (~80-90%). Tebentafusp (bispecific TCR-CD3 engager, HLA-A*02:01 restricted) is the first therapy with OS benefit in metastatic disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same neural crest melanocyte origin as cutaneous but distinct genomic biology — GNAQ/GNA11 drivers instead of BRAF in most cases, no UV signature, low TMB. BAP1 germline predisposes to a tumour spectrum including uveal melanoma, mesothelioma, RCC, and cutaneous melanoma. Hepatotropic metastasis reflects distinct lineage biology from cutaneous. Tebentafusp exploits gp100 melanocyte antigen restricted by HLA-A*02:01.

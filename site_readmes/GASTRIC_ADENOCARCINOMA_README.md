### tumor_microenvironment.notes
TCGA framework directly informs treatment  EBV+ and MSI-H are IO responders; GS (diffuse, CDH1-associated) may respond to CLDN18.2 or FGFR2b directed therapy. Peritoneal spread higher in diffuse-type.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
TCGA molecular subtypes reflect distinct lineage states: EBV+ (foveolar-type differentiation, PD-L1 amplified), MSI-H (intestinal-type, hypermutated), GS (genomically stable, diffuse-type with CDH1/RHOA alterations), CIN (chromosomal instability, intestinal-type). CDH1 germline causes hereditary diffuse gastric cancer + lobular breast cancer syndrome  same E-cadherin biology across these two cancers.

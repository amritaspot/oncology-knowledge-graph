# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: DLBCL_GCB

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
~30-40% of DLBCL by COO classification. Better prognosis than ABC/non-GCB with R-CHOP. Isolated MYC or BCL2 translocations do not by themselves reclassify DLBCL, but MYC + BCL2 rearrangements define high-grade B-cell lymphoma with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements; cases with MYC + BCL6 rearrangements are no longer included in that entity in WHO/ICC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
GCB subtype (~30-40% of DLBCL by COO classification). Polatuzumab-R-CHP (POLARIX) showed modest PFS benefit versus R-CHOP in IPI 2-5. CD19 CAR-T (axi-cel, liso-cel, tisa-cel) are approved for R/R LBCL/DLBCL in appropriate settings. Bispecifics (eg, epcoritamab, glofitamab) are approved for R/R DLBCL/LBCL. Loncastuximab tesirine (anti-CD19 ADC) is used in later lines.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
GCB subtype recapitulates germinal centre B-cell biology — this cell-of-origin classification (Hans algorithm IHC or gene expression) has therapeutic and prognostic implications. Recurrent genetic features enriched in GCB DLBCL include BCL2 translocation, EZH2, CREBBP, KMT2D, TNFRSF14, and GNA13 alterations; LymphGen clusters such as EZB further refine subtyping. Polatuzumab (anti-CD79b ADC), CD19 CAR-T, and bispecifics exploit retained B-cell antigen expression.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: TRACHEAL_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Rare tumour with histology-dependent behaviour. Squamous cell carcinoma more often shows aggressive local invasion and regional nodal spread, with distant metastases occurring later. Tracheal adenoid cystic carcinoma is typically indolent but prone to perineural/submucosal spread and late distant metastases, especially to lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Very rare (<0.2% of malignancies). Presents with airway obstruction. Adenoid cystic behaves indolently with late lung metastases (as per salivary ACC).

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Tracheal adenoid cystic and salivary adenoid cystic share recurrent MYB-pathway activation and biphasic differentiation — closely related lineage biology across anatomic sites. Tracheal SCC is associated with smoking like other upper aerodigestive SCCs. Both are rare (<0.2% of malignancies) but distinct entities.

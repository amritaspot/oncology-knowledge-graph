# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NSCLC_LARGE_CELL

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among all patients with large-cell lung carcinoma (n=2,832 in the SEER cohort). Denominator = all patients with this histology.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Brain; brain is the leading single-metastatic site in LCLC (23.5% of single-met cases).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.IMMUNE_HYPERSENSITIVITY.notes
Distant lymph nodes.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Category has shrunk with molecular reclassification. Most are now identified as adeno- or squamous-like on IHC/molecular. Behaves aggressively.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Diagnosis of exclusion in modern NSCLC pathology. Truly undifferentiated large cell tumours are increasingly rare with sensitive lineage IHC panels. Behaves aggressively — treated as NSCLC based on molecular subtype identified.

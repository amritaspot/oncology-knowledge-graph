# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AML_WITH_RECURRENT_GENETIC_ABNORMALITIES

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Includes t(8;21)/RUNX1-RUNX1T1 (favourable, often with granulocytic sarcoma), inv(16)/CBFB-MYH11 (favourable, myelomonocytic differentiation with abnormal eosinophils), NPM1-mutated, bZIP in-frame mutated CEBPA. Each subtype has distinct clinical patterns; extramedullary disease varies by molecular subtype.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Genetic subtype dominates management — favourable subsets (CBF, NPM1-mutated without adverse-risk features, biallelic CEBPA/bZIP) get standard chemo-based approaches; adverse subsets such as many KMT2Ar and NUP98r are often considered for transplant based on overall risk. Menin inhibitors (revumenib, ziftomenib) active in KMT2Ar and NPM1-mutant AML.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct genetic subtypes represent distinct diseases with characteristic maturation arrest patterns. NPM1 mutations cause aberrant cytoplasmic mislocalisation of NPM1 (from nucleolus) and are associated with HOX/MEIS1 programme activation; this biology overlaps with observed menin inhibitor sensitivity.

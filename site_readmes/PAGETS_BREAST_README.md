# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PAGETS_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
Usually associated with underlying DCIS or invasive ductal carcinoma; metastatic behaviour reflects underlying disease.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Almost always associated with underlying breast malignancy (most often DCIS and/or invasive carcinoma, typically in central/subareolar ducts). Nipple biopsy and breast imaging, often including MRI when mammography/ultrasound are unrevealing, are used to identify underlying disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Paget cells retain glandular (typically CK7+, often GCDFP-15+) rather than squamous phenotype despite intraepidermal location — this distinguishes from squamous disease. Most cases are associated with underlying ductal carcinoma, although truly isolated cases occur. HER2 is frequently overexpressed.

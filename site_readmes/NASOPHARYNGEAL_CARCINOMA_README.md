# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NASOPHARYNGEAL_CARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
EBV-driven biology; distinct pattern from UAT SCC. Distant metastases most commonly involve bone, followed by lung and liver in many contemporary series; multi-site metastasis is common. Patterns vary by cohort, and SEER-based analyses have reported bone as the leading site.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
EBV-driven immune activation. PD-L1 expression is often increased, but variable by assay and cutoff. This likely contributes to sensitivity to immunotherapy in R/M NPC. Immunosuppressive features include EBV-encoded proteins (especially LMP1) driving PD-L1 upregulation.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Endemic (southern China, SE Asia) EBV-driven non-keratinizing/undifferentiated form is a distinct entity from sporadic keratinizing SCC. Dietary nitrosamine-related exposures interact with EBV in endemic populations. EBV latency programme is latency II (including EBNA1, LMP1, and LMP2 expression).

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: HEPATOSPLENIC_T_CELL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Usually of b3b4 T-cell origin, with a minority b1b2 variant. Association with prolonged immunosuppression (post-transplant, IBD on thiopurines with or without anti-TNF therapy). Aggressive; poor prognosis.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Typically no or minimal lymphadenopathy despite advanced disease. Hepatosplenomegaly with cytopenias. Aggressive with poor prognosis. Allo-HSCT is the only potentially curative approach in eligible patients. Isochromosome 7q [i(7q)] is characteristic.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
b3b4 T-cell lineage explains the distinctive sinusoidal infiltration pattern in liver, spleen, and bone marrow. Typically little or no lymphadenopathy despite advanced disease. Immunosuppression association (post-transplant, IBD on thiopurines with or without anti-TNF therapy) is well recognized. Isochromosome 7q [i(7q)] is a characteristic cytogenetic abnormality. Very poor prognosis.

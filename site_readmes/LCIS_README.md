# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LCIS

### metastasis_patterns_note
In situ; non-metastatic. Marker of elevated bilateral breast cancer risk (~1-2%/year).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Not obligate precursor but bilateral risk marker (~1-2%/year). Pleomorphic variant behaves more aggressively and gets excised.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Classical LCIS is a risk marker for future bilateral cancer (~1-2%/year — either lobular or ductal) and can also serve as a non-obligate precursor to invasive lobular carcinoma. Pleomorphic LCIS has more aggressive appearance and is treated more like DCIS (excision with clear margins). Same E-cadherin biology as invasive lobular.

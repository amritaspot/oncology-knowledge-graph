# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GIST

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinct KIT/PDGFRA-driven mesenchymal biology. Metastatic pattern: liver and peritoneum are the dominant sites (most common), while bone/lung/lymph nodes are uncommon. Notably rarely spreads to lymph nodes — a distinguishing feature. Not covered in Riihimäki 2018 (aggregated under 'Other'), diSibio 2008 (not enumerated as separate category), or Budczies 2015 (excludes sarcomas).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
SDH-deficient GISTs (paediatric, epithelioid, gastric, relatively imatinib-insensitive) have distinct biology. TKI-driven biology dominates therapy.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
ICC lineage is defining — the tumour recapitulates the biology of the enteric pacemaker system. KIT/PDGFRA activating mutations drive most GISTs (imatinib-responsive). SDH-deficient GISTs (paediatric, Carney triad, Carney-Stratakis) are wild-type for KIT/PDGFRA and have distinct behaviour.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AITL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Follicular T helper cell origin. TET2, DNMT3A, IDH2, RHOA mutations. Aggressive.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Follicular T helper (TFH) cell origin. TET2, DNMT3A, IDH2, RHOA mutations define epigenetic dysregulation biology — responses to epigenetic therapy have been reported in AITL/TFH-type PTCL. Renamed 'nodal TFH-cell lymphoma, angioimmunoblastic-type' in WHO 2022.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
TFH lineage explains distinctive TME — neoplastic TFH cells recruit and support EBV+ B-cell expansion and follicular dendritic cell hyperplasia. Same epigenetic mutations (TET2, DNMT3A, IDH2) are recurrent in clonal haematopoiesis and myeloid malignancies — same pathway biology across lineages. Renamed 'nodal TFH-cell lymphoma, angioimmunoblastic-type' in WHO 2022 reflects cell of origin biology.

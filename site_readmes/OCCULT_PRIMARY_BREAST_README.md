# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: OCCULT_PRIMARY_BREAST

### metastasis_patterns_note
Classically presents with axillary nodal metastasis without an identifiable breast primary on clinical exam and standard breast imaging; MRI often detects an otherwise occult ipsilateral breast lesion. Managed as breast cancer when pathology and clinical context support breast origin.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Breast MRI frequently detects a small occult primary; if no primary is found, treatment typically includes axillary management plus ipsilateral whole-breast treatment, and systemic therapy is given according to receptor/HER2 status. Managed according to node-positive breast cancer paradigms.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Clinical entity rather than lineage-defined subtype. GATA3, mammaglobin, and GCDFP-15 can support breast origin in nodal biopsy, interpreted with morphology and ER/PR/HER2. MRI often identifies a small primary. Treated as node-positive breast cancer with systemic therapy per receptor status.

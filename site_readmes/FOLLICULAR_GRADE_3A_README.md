# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: FOLLICULAR_GRADE_3A

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Usually presents in lymph nodes; bone marrow involvement can occur but is less frequent than in grades 1-2. Treated as indolent FL by many groups, although management is variably approached and some data support anthracycline-containing or DLBCL-like therapy in selected cases. Behaviour intermediate.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Treated as indolent FL by many groups, although management remains debated and some evidence supports DLBCL-like or anthracycline-containing approaches in selected cases. Behaviour intermediate. Same general biomarker workup as low-grade FL, while recognizing that BCL2 rearrangement/expression may be less frequent than in grades 1-2.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same underlying germinal centre biology as low-grade FL with increased proliferation. Behaviour intermediate. Same general biomarker workup as low-grade FL, with recognition that canonical FL drivers such as IGH::BCL2 may be less consistently present than in grades 1-2.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MULTIPLE_MYELOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Extramedullary myeloma (EMM) at relapse portends poor prognosis. Skeletal survey / whole-body low-dose CT / MRI or PET-CT for bone assessment. Ig type (IgG > IgA > light chain only).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Modern therapy landscape: dara-VRd (PERSEUS, GRIFFIN) frontline; BCMA CAR-T (ide-cel — KarMMa; cilta-cel — CARTITUDE) approved for R/R; BCMA×CD3 (teclistamab, elranatamab) and GPRC5D×CD3 (talquetamab) bispecifics; venetoclax highly active in t(11;14). t(11;14) is BCL2-dependent — targetable subset.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Terminal plasma cell differentiation explains susceptibility to targeting plasma cell-specific antigens. Marrow niche dependency (IL-6, VEGF, CXCL12) drives disease biology. Extramedullary disease with plasmablastic morphology represents lineage regression and worse prognosis. t(11;14) MM shows BCL2 dependency — venetoclax highly active.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AL_AMYLOIDOSIS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Mayo staging uses cardiac biomarkers and free light chains (troponin, NT-proBNP, difference in involved/uninvolved free light chains in revised systems). Cardiac involvement dominates prognosis. Rapid diagnosis critical — bortezomib-based regimens and daratumumab-CyBorD are standard first-line approaches.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Cardiac involvement dominates prognosis. Rapid diagnosis critical. Distinguishing AL from ATTR is essential — bone scintigraphy (99mTc-DPD/PYP) grade 2-3 uptake with negative monoclonal workup supports ATTR (often no biopsy needed). Advanced cardiac stage carries very poor survival without early effective therapy.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Small underlying clone can drive severe organ damage through amyloidogenic light chain deposition. Biased clonal Ig light chain gene usage contributes to organ tropism. t(11;14) enrichment relative to MM supports biologic rationale for venetoclax, but this is not yet a universally established standard of care in AL. dara-CyBorD (ANDROMEDA) is standard first-line.

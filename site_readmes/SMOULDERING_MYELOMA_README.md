# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SMOULDERING_MYELOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Progression to MM is approximately 10% per year for the first 5 years overall, then declines thereafter. High-risk SMM may benefit from early intervention in selected patients or clinical trials; lenalidomide-based approaches have delayed progression in randomized studies.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Progression to MM is approximately 10% per year for the first 5 years overall. The Mayo 2/20/20 model uses M-spike >20 g/L, BM plasma cells >20%, and involved/uninvolved FLC ratio >20 for risk stratification; increasing number of factors confers increasing risk. >1 focal MRI lesion or biomarker criteria under SLiM-CRAB upstage to active MM.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PANCREATIC_NET_G3

### metastasis_patterns_note
See PANCREATIC_NET_G1; distinct from NEC despite high Ki-67 — often retains SSTR expression and may respond to NET-directed therapy, but SSTR expression is variable and not universally retained.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinct from NEC — SSTR2 may be retained, allowing consideration of PRRT and SSA in selected cases. Alkylator-based chemotherapy such as temozolomide-based regimens is generally more active than platinum-etoposide, which is standard for NEC; platinum may still be considered in some high-proliferation cases.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Critical WHO distinction from poorly differentiated pancreatic NEC. Molecular biology commonly overlaps with well-differentiated pNET (for example MEN1, DAXX, ATRX) rather than NEC (TP53/RB1). Chemotherapy choice often favors temozolomide-based or other NET-directed approaches over platinum-etoposide used for NEC.

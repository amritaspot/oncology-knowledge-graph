# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MESOTHELIOMA_PERICARDIAL

### metastasis_patterns_note
No source harvested; extremely rare tumour, case-report and small case-series data only. Metastatic patterns are not well defined; disease is typically dominated by local invasion within the pericardium/mediastinum and malignant pericardial effusion.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Extremely rare. Presents with pericardial effusion, tamponade. Poor prognosis due to late diagnosis and difficult surgical access.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same mesothelial lineage principles as other mesotheliomas but distinct anatomic constraints. Poor prognosis is anatomically driven rather than biologically distinct — pericardial location limits surgical resection and diagnosis is often delayed.

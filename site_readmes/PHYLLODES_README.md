# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PHYLLODES

### metastasis_patterns_note
Not a carcinoma; sarcoma-like behaviour. Malignant phyllodes metastasize predominantly haematogenously (lung most common; bone and other visceral sites also reported), not via lymphatics; nodal spread is rare.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Behaves as a sarcoma when malignant  wide excision (no routine ALND). Metastasises hematogenously, most often to lung; bone and other visceral metastases can occur. Rare heterologous elements (liposarcomatous, chondrosarcomatous, rhabdomyosarcomatous) may develop.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct from other breast cancers because of stromal (mesenchymal) origin  behaves as a sarcoma with predominantly hematogenous metastasis and rare lymph node spread. Wide excision is standard; axillary dissection is not routine. MED12 mutations are common, and TERT promoter alterations are recurrent, particularly in borderline/malignant tumours. Metastatic disease is generally managed with sarcoma-type regimens.

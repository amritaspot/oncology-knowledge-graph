# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ERDHEIM_CHESTER

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Non-Langerhans histiocytosis. BRAF V600E in ~50-60%; other MAPK pathway alterations are present in many of the remaining cases. Vemurafenib and cobimetinib are highly active in appropriately selected patients. Predominantly an adult disease; overlaps with LCH histologically and molecularly in some cases.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Non-Langerhans histiocytosis. Vemurafenib is approved for BRAF V600 mutation-positive ECD. Cobimetinib is approved for adults with histiocytic neoplasms and supports the central role of MAPK-driven biology. Predominantly an adult disease with characteristic multiorgan involvement.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Non-Langerhans macrophage/histiocytic lineage distinguishes from LCH — with shared MAPK biology across related histiocytic disorders. Vemurafenib is approved for BRAF V600 mutation-positive ECD. Cobimetinib is approved for adults with histiocytic neoplasms. Characteristic multi-organ involvement (long-bone osteosclerosis, 'hairy kidney', 'coated aorta') reflects distinctive tissue tropism.

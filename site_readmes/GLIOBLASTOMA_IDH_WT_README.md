# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GLIOBLASTOMA_IDH_WT

### metastasis_patterns_note
Extraneural metastasis is very rare (<1%, often cited around 0.40.5%); when reported, lung/pleura, bone, lymph nodes, and liver are among the most frequent sites. VP shunt-associated extraneural spread has been historically described. Recurrence is typically local, with subependymal/CSF dissemination also recognized.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Immune-cold TME explains poor IO monotherapy response 1 trials mostly negative (CheckMate 143, 498, 548). MGMT methylation predicts temozolomide benefit. TTFields (tumour-treating fields) is an approved adjunct.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Cellular heterogeneity with GSC hierarchy is defining biology. TCGA subtypes reflect underlying lineage states with therapeutic implications. EGFR amplification/variant III mutation is characteristic of a major subset. MGMT promoter methylation predicts temozolomide benefit through impaired repair of O6-methylguanine DNA damage.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: B_ALL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
CNS prophylaxis mandatory (intrathecal chemotherapy ± cranial RT in selected protocols). Testicular examination at relapse. MRD-negative CR is the key treatment goal.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
CD19 CAR-T (tisagenlecleucel, brexucabtagene) and blinatumomab dramatically improved outcomes in R/R disease. MRD-directed therapy is standard. CNS prophylaxis (IT chemo ± cranial RT in selected protocols) mandatory. Testis is a classic relapse sanctuary.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
CD19, CD20, CD22 are B-cell lineage markers exploited therapeutically: CD19 CAR-T (tisa-cel, brexu-cel), blinatumomab (CD19×CD3), inotuzumab (CD22 ADC). CNS and testis are sanctuary sites requiring specific prophylaxis. Lineage switch to AML under CD19-directed therapy pressure represents an emerging resistance mechanism.

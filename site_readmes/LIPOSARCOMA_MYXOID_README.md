# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LIPOSARCOMA_MYXOID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinctive extrapulmonary pattern — soft tissues and serosal surfaces (including retroperitoneum), bone/spine, and abdomen/pelvis — in addition to lung. FUS-DDIT3 fusion driver.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinctive extrapulmonary metastasis pattern (soft tissue/serosal sites including retroperitoneum, bone/spine, abdomen/pelvis) — whole-body MRI has been advocated in advanced disease because of the atypical metastatic distribution. Radiosensitive (unlike other liposarcomas). NY-ESO-1 TCR-T cell therapies in trial.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct lineage biology from WD/DD LPS despite shared 'liposarcoma' name — different fusion, different clinical behaviour (including distinctive extrapulmonary metastasis pattern). NY-ESO-1 highly expressed enables NY-ESO-1 TCR-T therapy (letetresgene autoleucel, afami-cel). Radiosensitive (unlike other liposarcomas).

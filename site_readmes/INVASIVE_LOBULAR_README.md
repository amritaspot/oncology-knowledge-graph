# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: INVASIVE_LOBULAR

### metastasis_patterns_note
See INVASIVE_DUCTAL_NST — Riihimäki 2018 aggregate data applies with caution because it is not histology-specific. Lobular carcinoma has distinct organotropism versus ductal: higher rates of peritoneal/retroperitoneal, GI (including stomach — mimicking linitis plastica), ovarian, uterine/gynecologic, bone/bone marrow, orbital, and leptomeningeal metastasis; lower rate of lung parenchymal spread but relatively more pleural/serosal involvement.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
E-cadherin loss produces discohesive infiltration pattern and can underlie diffuse infiltrative metastases such as gastric linitis plastica-like involvement. Less inflamed TME may contribute to limited immunotherapy responsiveness in unselected disease. Distinct metastatic tropism includes peritoneum/retroperitoneum, GI, gynecologic organs, serosal sites, orbit, and leptomeninges.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
E-cadherin loss (most often due to CDH1 inactivation with accompanying 16q loss, and/or less commonly other alterations affecting the cadherin-catenin complex) is the defining molecular event and produces the characteristic morphology and metastatic pattern (peritoneal, GI, gynecologic, leptomeningeal, orbital/serosal sites). Germline CDH1 pathogenic variants cause hereditary diffuse gastric cancer and increase lobular breast cancer risk.

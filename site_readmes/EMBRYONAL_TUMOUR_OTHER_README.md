# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: EMBRYONAL_TUMOUR_OTHER

### metastasis_patterns_note
Includes ETMR, CNS neuroblastoma, FOXR2-activated, CNS tumour with BCOR internal tandem duplication, medulloepithelioma, and others. CSF dissemination/leptomeningeal spread can occur; craniospinal staging is required.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rare paediatric CNS tumours. Molecular classification via methylation profiling drives modern diagnosis. Aggressive; often requires high-intensity multimodal therapy.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Category comprises heterogeneous rare paediatric CNS embryonal tumours united by molecular classification. Methylation profiling has revolutionised diagnosis. DICER1 germline predisposes to ETMR, pleuropulmonary blastoma, and other cancers. Aggressive; often requires high-intensity multimodal therapy.

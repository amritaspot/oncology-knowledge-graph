# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NSCLC_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among all patients with NSCLC adenocarcinoma (n=75,231 in the SEER cohort; ~38% overall metastatic rate). Denominator = all patients with this histology, not just metastatic ones. Includes only the four extrathoracic sites recorded in SEER (bone, brain, liver, distant lymph node); intrathoracic spread and other visceral mets are not enumerated here.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; most common extrathoracic site in NSCLC-adeno.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Brain.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.IMMUNE_HYPERSENSITIVITY.notes
Distant lymph nodes (categorised here under immune system for corpus consistency).

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
STK11 and KEAP1 mutations produce immune-cold TME and predict IO resistance. Driver-positive tumours (EGFR, ALK) typically have less inflamed TME. Smokers have higher TMB and neoantigen load — more inflamed.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
TTF-1 and napsin A are lineage-defining markers of pneumocytic differentiation. KRAS G12C driver is most common in smokers; EGFR mutations in never-smokers of East Asian descent. Neuroendocrine transformation after 3rd-gen TKI (osimertinib) is a distinctive resistance mechanism reflecting lineage plasticity in the same clonal background.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ONCOCYTIC_THYROID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Historically classified with follicular; clinically more aggressive than non-oncocytic follicular thyroid carcinoma in many series; distant metastases most often involve lung and bone. Reduced radioiodine avidity is common, complicating treatment.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinctive mitochondrial biology (mtDNA mutations). Often loses iodine avidity  complicating radioiodine therapy. Multikinase inhibitors may be used for metastatic radioiodine-refractory disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinctive mitochondrial biology with widespread aneuploidy and recurrent chromosome 7 gain. Historically classified with follicular thyroid carcinoma but now recognised as molecularly distinct. Poor radioiodine uptake can make multikinase inhibitors such as lenvatinib important for advanced radioiodine-refractory disease.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MEDULLARY_THYROID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinct C-cell origin (calcitonin-producing). Nodal spread early; distant metastasis most commonly to liver, lung, and bone. Liver metastases may show a diffuse/miliary pattern on imaging. Not iodine-avid.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
C-cell origin distinguishes from other thyroid cancers. Amyloid stains with Congo red.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Unique among thyroid cancers — arises from calcitonin-producing C cells, not thyroid follicular epithelium. This explains distinct biomarker set (calcitonin, CEA, not thyroglobulin) and lack of iodine avidity. Same RET-driven biology in MEN2 kindreds affects other tissues such as adrenal medulla (pheochromocytoma).

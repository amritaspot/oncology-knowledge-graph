# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GI_NET_G1

### metastasis_patterns_note
Budczies 2015 includes neuroendocrine tumours as one of 16 major cancer types but aggregates all grades and sites. Clinical pattern for small bowel (midgut) G1 NETs: liver (dominant, often the presenting feature in functional tumours with carcinoid syndrome), mesenteric lymph nodes, peritoneal spread/mesenteric deposits common; bone metastases occur, typically in advanced disease and may be sclerotic/osteoblastic. Somatostatin receptor imaging (68Ga-DOTATATE PET/CT, where available) is standard staging for well-differentiated SSTR-positive NETs.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
SSTR2 expression drives theranostic strategy — DOTATATE imaging and PRRT. Carcinoid heart disease (right-sided valvular fibrosis) from serotonin in midgut NETs with liver metastases. IO not typically effective.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Site-of-origin determines hormone production and clinical syndrome: midgut serotonin producers can cause carcinoid syndrome (flushing, diarrhoea, right-sided valve disease), classically when hepatic metastases allow vasoactive products to escape first-pass metabolism. SSTR2 expression is often retained (in contrast to poorly differentiated NEC) enabling PRRT.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SYSTEMIC_MASTOCYTOSIS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
KIT D816V mutation is the canonical driver in most adult SM cases. Disease ranges from indolent (ISM) to advanced forms (ASM, SM-AHN, MCL). Midostaurin and avapritinib are established therapies for advanced disease; avapritinib is also approved for indolent SM in some jurisdictions.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
KIT D816V is typically imatinib-resistant (mutation impairs drug binding). Avapritinib is highly active against KIT D816V-mutant SM and is approved for advanced SM; midostaurin also has activity. Symptom-directed therapy includes H1/H2 blockers and mast cell-directed supportive measures.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Mast cell mediator release (histamine, tryptase, prostaglandins and other mediators) drives systemic symptoms — flushing, urticaria, anaphylaxis, diarrhoea, hypotension. KIT D816V biology is shared across many mastocytosis subtypes, while severity is influenced by disease burden, organ damage, and additional mutations. Avapritinib targets KIT D816V selectively; imatinib is generally ineffective against KIT D816V-mutated disease.

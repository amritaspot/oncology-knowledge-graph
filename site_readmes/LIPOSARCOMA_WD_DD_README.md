# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LIPOSARCOMA_WD_DD

### metastasis_patterns_note
Sarcomas explicitly excluded from Riihimki 2018 and Budczies 2015 cohorts. WDLPS almost never metastasises (local recurrence dominates). DDLPS metastasises most commonly to lung; reported distant metastasis rates are variable and generally substantially lower than 30% overall, with liver and bone less common. Retroperitoneal DDLPS often recurs locally rather than distantly.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
MDM2 amplification is the defining event and enables MDM2 inhibitor trials (milademetan, brigimadlin). Retroperitoneal location often precludes complete resection  local recurrence rather than distant metastasis is the dominant problem.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
MDM2 amplification is a defining lineage-relevant event  MDM2 IHC and FISH distinguish from lipoma. Recognition of the shared MDM2 amplicon enabled acceptance that WD and DD LPS are the same disease with differentiation switching. MDM2 inhibitors (milademetan, brigimadlin) exploit this dependency.

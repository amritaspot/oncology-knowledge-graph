# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: EPENDYMOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
CSF dissemination is the primary distant spread pattern; craniospinal imaging is required for staging/workup in intracranial ependymoma. Extraneural metastasis is very rare.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Molecular subgroup > histologic grade for prognosis in modern WHO CNS5 classification. Location + molecular subgroup drive treatment intensity. CSF dissemination is the primary distant spread pattern.

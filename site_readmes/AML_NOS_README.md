# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AML_NOS

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
BM/PB is the disease home. Extramedullary disease is more common with monocytic differentiation and in some genetic subtypes. Hyperleukocytic disease (>50-100K WBC) causes leukostasis in lung and CNS.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Genetic risk stratification (ELN 2022) drives management more than morphology in the modern era. MRD-directed therapy is standard. Venetoclax-based combinations (VIALE-A) transformed unfit-patient management. Targeted therapies proliferating (FLT3, IDH1/2, menin inhibitors).

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
LSC hierarchy is fundamental, but no single universal immunophenotype identifies all AML LSCs. Venetoclax + azacitidine appears to target oxidative phosphorylation-dependent LSC populations. Site of maturation arrest often correlates with genetic driver (for example KMT2A-rearranged AML frequently shows monocytic differentiation, while CBF AML often retains granulocytic maturation features).

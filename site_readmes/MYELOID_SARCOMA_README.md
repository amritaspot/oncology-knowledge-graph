# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: MYELOID_SARCOMA

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Extramedullary manifestation of AML — may present without concurrent marrow disease (isolated myeloid sarcoma) or precede/accompany overt AML. Treated as AML.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
May present as isolated mass without marrow involvement — still treated as AML with systemic chemotherapy (not local RT alone) because isolated lesions commonly progress to systemic AML if untreated systemically. Core-binding factor AML, including t(8;21), is associated with myeloid sarcoma.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same disease as AML with distinctive tissue tropism. May present as isolated extramedullary mass without marrow involvement (or precede systemic AML). Still treated as AML with systemic chemotherapy — local RT alone insufficient. Core-binding factor AML, including t(8;21), has a recognized predilection for extramedullary presentation.

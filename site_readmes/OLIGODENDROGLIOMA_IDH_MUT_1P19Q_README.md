# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: OLIGODENDROGLIOMA_IDH_MUT_1P19Q

### metastasis_patterns_note
As per ASTROCYTOMA_IDH_MUT — extraneural metastasis exceedingly rare. Better prognosis; local recurrence dominates natural history.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Best prognosis among diffuse gliomas — median survival often >10-15 years for grade 2. RT plus PCV is the best-established adjuvant approach in higher-risk disease; temozolomide is also widely used. TERT promoter mutations are very common, while CIC and FUBP1 mutations are common but not near-universal.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
1p/19q whole-arm co-deletion is defining alongside IDH mutation. OPC-like lineage programs likely contribute to distinctive oligodendroglial morphology and biology.

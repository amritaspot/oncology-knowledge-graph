# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: AML_WITH_MDS_RELATED_CHANGES

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Adverse-risk AML defined by prior MDS/MDS-MPN history, MDS-related cytogenetic abnormalities, or MDS-related gene mutations. Extramedullary disease is not a defining feature. Often preceded by MDS or MDS/MPN.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Poor outcomes with conventional intensive induction overall. CPX-351 is specifically approved for adults with newly diagnosed therapy-related AML or AML with myelodysplasia-related changes. Venetoclax plus a hypomethylating agent is a standard option for patients unfit for intensive therapy. Allogeneic transplant is strongly considered when feasible given adverse biology.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Represents clonal evolution from MDS or MDS-like biology. Chronic ineffective haematopoiesis with multilineage dysplasia often distinguishes it from de novo AML. Poorer response to intensive chemotherapy reflects adverse leukemia stem-cell biology distinct from many de novo AML subsets.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PANCREATIC_NET_G1

### metastasis_patterns_note
Liver-dominant metastatic pattern, with regional lymph nodes and liver the most common sites; bone and peritoneal metastases can also occur in advanced disease. Functional subtypes (insulinoma, gastrinoma) have distinctive clinical syndromes; metastatic pattern is generally similar, although insulinomas are often localized and less frequently metastatic.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinct from PDAC — often indolent, hypervascular (arterial-enhancing on imaging), SSTR2-driven. mTOR pathway alterations are recurrent in pNETs and everolimus is active. Functional syndromes often the presenting feature.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct lineage from pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma despite same organ. MEN1, DAXX, ATRX and mTOR-pathway gene alterations are characteristic (distinct from KRAS-driven PDAC). Functional tumours produce clinical syndromes (Whipple triad in insulinoma, ZES in gastrinoma, glucagonoma syndrome). Hypervascular imaging pattern reflects islet cell origin.

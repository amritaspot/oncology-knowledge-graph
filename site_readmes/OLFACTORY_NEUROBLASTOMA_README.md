# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: OLFACTORY_NEUROBLASTOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
No specific source harvested; cervical lymph node involvement is the most common regional metastatic pattern; distant metastases most often involve bone and lung, with liver and central nervous system less commonly reported in case series.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Neural/neuroendocrine differentiation. Sustentacular cells resemble those in paraganglioma. TME poorly studied.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct from most other sinonasal tumours because of neuroectodermal/neural differentiation rather than surface epithelial differentiation. Sustentacular S100+ cells at nest periphery are a characteristic diagnostic feature. Somatostatin receptor expression can enable DOTATATE PET and, in selected cases, PRRT.

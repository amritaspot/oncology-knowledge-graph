# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: APPENDICEAL_MUCINOUS_NEOPLASM

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinctive peritoneal-dominant spread — pseudomyxoma peritonei (PMP) with widespread peritoneal mucinous deposits. Extra-peritoneal metastasis (including liver or lung) is uncommon and generally associated with high-grade/invasive disease; localized scalloping or surface involvement of visceral organs may occur as part of peritoneal dissemination. Treated primarily with cytoreductive surgery; HIPEC is commonly used in selected patients.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
PMP grade drives prognosis and management. Cytoreductive surgery is standard in selected patients; HIPEC is commonly incorporated. Low-grade disease may allow long survival even with extensive peritoneal spread.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
LAMN and HAMN are distinct from goblet cell adenocarcinoma and colonic-type appendiceal adenocarcinoma. Well-differentiated biology helps explain prolonged survival in some patients despite extensive peritoneal disease. KRAS and GNAS mutations are characteristic. Cytoreductive surgery, often with HIPEC, is standard for selected patients with PMP.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: EXTRANODAL_NK_T_NASAL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
EBV-driven. Angiocentric, angiodestructive. Higher incidence in East Asia and Latin America. Asparaginase-containing regimens; RT for early localised disease.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
EBV-driven with plasma EBV DNA quantitation for monitoring. Higher incidence in East Asia and Latin America. Asparaginase-containing regimens (SMILE, DDGP) foundation of therapy. RT for early localised nasal disease. Extra-nasal presentation is more aggressive.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
NK-cell lineage helps explain sensitivity to asparaginase-containing therapy, but the established rationale is extracellular asparagine dependence rather than a consensus ASS1/arginine-depletion mechanism. EBV-driven biology is essentially universal. Angiocentric, angiodestructive infiltrate reflects distinctive tumour-vascular interactions. Higher incidence in East Asian and Latin American populations.

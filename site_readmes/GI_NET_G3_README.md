# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GI_NET_G3

### metastasis_patterns_note
Well-differentiated but high Ki-67. Metastatic pattern generally resembles lower-grade GI NETs with liver-dominant spread, but with more aggressive clinical course and faster progression.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Critical distinction from NEC: retained SSTR2 expression is common and may allow PRRT and sometimes SSA use, but this is not universal. Chemotherapy responses to platinum-etoposide are generally lower than in NEC. This category exists because of misclassification concerns leading to overtreatment.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Critical WHO 2019/2022 distinction — well-differentiated NET G3 must be separated from poorly differentiated NEC despite similar Ki-67. G3 NET often retains SSTR expression (supporting SSA/PRRT in selected cases), whereas NEC more often shows TP53/RB1 alteration and less consistent SSTR expression. Molecular biology is more similar to well-differentiated NET, including MEN1, DAXX, ATRX alterations in pancreatic primaries; site-specific GI data are more limited. Practical consequence: therapy differs from NEC, and platinum-etoposide is generally less effective in G3 NET than in NEC.

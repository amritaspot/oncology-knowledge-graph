# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: APL

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Distinctive DIC/coagulopathy at presentation is the major early mortality risk — not from disease bulk but from bleeding. Curable with ATRA + arsenic in most cases. CNS relapse risk is increased in hyperleukocytic/high-risk disease; routine CNS prophylaxis is not universally standard for all such patients and is center/protocol dependent.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinctive coagulopathy (DIC) at presentation is the major early mortality risk — bleeding, not disease bulk. ATRA + arsenic trioxide is curative in >90% of non-high-risk patients without chemotherapy (Lo-Coco APL0406). PLZF-RARA variant is resistant to ATRA and arsenic trioxide.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Paradigmatic example of differentiation-based therapy — ATRA induces terminal differentiation of arrested promyelocytes rather than killing them. PML-RARA repression of retinoid signalling explains both differentiation arrest and ATRA sensitivity. Arsenic trioxide targets the PML moiety for degradation. Distinctive DIC/coagulopathy from procoagulant activity associated with arrested promyelocytes.

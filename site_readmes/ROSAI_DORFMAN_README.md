# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ROSAI_DORFMAN

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Emperipolesis on histology. Familial/syndromic cases can be associated with SLC29A3 mutations. Often self-limiting; observation or symptomatic treatment; targeted therapy for MAPK-pathway-altered refractory cases.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Massive painless cervical lymphadenopathy is classic. Often self-limiting; observation for asymptomatic disease. MEK inhibition can be considered for refractory disease. Familial/syndromic cases associated with SLC29A3 warrant genetic counselling/testing.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinctive S100+/CD68+/CD1a- phenotype with emperipolesis is characteristic. Shared MAPK-pathway biology with other histiocytic neoplasms may underlie MEK inhibitor activity in selected cases. Familial/syndromic SLC29A3-associated disease has distinct pathogenesis. Often self-limiting; observation for asymptomatic disease.

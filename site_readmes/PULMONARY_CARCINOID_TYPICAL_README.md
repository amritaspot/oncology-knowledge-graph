# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PULMONARY_CARCINOID_TYPICAL

### metastasis_patterns_note
Typical and atypical carcinoid are grouped as TC+AC in the Nature 2024 SEER analysis (Chen et al., Sci Rep 2024). Reported multi-organ metastasis rate 28.7% among metastatic TC+AC; sole liver metastasis in 21.5% of metastatic TC+AC cases. Site-specific breakdown by typical vs atypical not separately harvested. In general, typical pulmonary carcinoid metastasizes less often than atypical carcinoid; when metastatic, regional lymph nodes, liver, bone, and brain are recognized sites.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Indolent behaviour. Surgical resection curative in most. Somatostatin receptor expression drives theranostic and PRRT strategies.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Well-differentiated neuroendocrine biology explains frequent SSTR expression (target for imaging and PRRT), low proliferative activity, and indolent course. Molecularly distinct from SCLC/LCNEC — MEN1, chromatin-remodeling gene alterations (including ARID1A) and EIF1AX have been reported, whereas TP53/RB1 co-alteration is not the dominant pattern. No recurrent targetable driver fusion is established as a hallmark.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GI_NEC_POORLY_DIFF

### metastasis_patterns_note
Distinct from well-differentiated NETs. Aggressive, small-cell-like biology. Widely metastatic at diagnosis  liver, distant lymph nodes/peritoneum, lung, and bone are common; brain metastases can occur but are less common than in SCLC. Treated with platinum-etoposide like SCLC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Treated as SCLC  platinum-etoposide is standard first-line therapy. MSI-H subset (higher than well-differentiated NETs in some GI sites, especially colorectum) may benefit from immune checkpoint blockade. Loss or low expression of SSTR2 reduces utility of DOTATATE-based strategies.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Molecular biology often parallels SCLC (frequent TP53 alteration with common RB1 inactivation) rather than well-differentiated NET (MEN1/DAXX/ATRX). Reduced SSTR2 expression helps differentiate from well-differentiated G3 NET and often limits PRRT/SSA benefit. MiNEN (mixed neuroendocrine and adenocarcinoma) supports common progenitor origin with divergent lineage differentiation.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SALIVARY_ACINIC_CELL

### metastasis_patterns_note
Generally an indolent salivary gland carcinoma with uncommon distant metastasis; when metastases occur, reported sites include cervical lymph nodes, lung, and bone. High-grade transformation is associated with increased risk of nodal and distant spread.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Generally indolent; TME features not well-studied. Lymphocytic infiltrate reported in some subtypes.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
NR4A3 activation is a hallmark event in acinic cell carcinoma, most often through recurrent rearrangements/enhancer hijacking at the NR4A3 locus rather than a single uniform fusion partner. Nuclear NR4A3 IHC is diagnostically useful. Parotid predominance reflects normal serous acinar distribution.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: POEMS_SYNDROME

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
VEGF elevation is characteristic and helps diagnosis. Treated with plasma cell-directed therapy (lenalidomide-based) or autologous transplant.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Distinctive from MM: osteosclerotic (not lytic) lesions, lambda light chain restriction, VEGF elevation. Plasma cell-directed therapy (lenalidomide-based) or autologous transplant. Neuropathy is disabling and often improves slowly with treatment.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinctive plasma cell biology explains multi-organ syndrome — VEGF overproduction associated with the small clone drives polyneuropathy, organomegaly, endocrinopathy, skin changes. Lambda light chain restriction is highly characteristic. Osteosclerotic (not lytic) lesions are characteristic. Distinct entity from typical MM despite same plasma cell lineage.

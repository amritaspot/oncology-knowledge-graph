# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: POORLY_DIFFERENTIATED_THYROID

### metastasis_patterns_note
Aggressive; distant metastasis commonly to lung and bone, and less commonly CNS/brain; often loses RAI-avidity.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Often arises from dedifferentiation of PTC/FTC. Loss of iodine avidity. RAI-refractory disease requires TKI (lenvatinib, sorafenib) or targeted therapy per driver.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Turin criteria capture the morphologic signature of this dedifferentiation state (solid/trabecular/insular growth, convoluted nuclei, mitoses, necrosis). Represents a therapeutic window before full anaplastic transformation.

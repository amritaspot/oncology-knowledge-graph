# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: INVASIVE_DUCTAL_NST

### metastasis_patterns.aggregation_note
Riihimäki source combines all breast histologies AND all molecular subtypes. Molecular subtype tropism differs materially: HR+/HER2- favours bone-only; HER2+ and TNBC have more visceral and brain metastasis. Male breast cancer excluded from this cohort.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; dominant metastatic site in breast cancer.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.PULMONARY.notes
Lung.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Nervous system/brain; higher in HER2+ and TNBC subtypes.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.PULMONARY_PLEURA.notes
Pleura; seen in breast cancer.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.DERMATOLOGIC.notes
Skin (including chest wall/cutaneous metastasis).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL.notes
Peritoneum; more common in lobular subtype.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.GASTROINTESTINAL_OTHER.notes
Other GI; lobular has GI tropism.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.REPRODUCTIVE_GONADAL.notes
Ovary.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement_pct.ENDOCRINE.notes
Adrenal gland.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Stromal TIL (sTIL) scoring is standardized mainly in TNBC, where it is prognostic and associated with pCR to neoadjuvant chemotherapy; in HER2-positive disease TILs are also prognostic/predictive, but routine clinical decision-making is less standardized.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
PAM50 intrinsic subtyping (luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched, basal-like, normal-like) reflects lineage state and drives biology and therapy. GATA3, FOXA1, ESR1 form a luminal-defining transcriptional circuit. Breast cancer stem-cell phenotypes such as CD44+/CD24- have been described, but are not routine clinical biomarkers.

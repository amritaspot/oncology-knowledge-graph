# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: NSCLC_SQUAMOUS

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among all patients with NSCLC squamous cell carcinoma (n=37,179 in the SEER cohort). Denominator = all patients with this histology. SCC has the lowest distant metastasis rate among lung cancer subtypes.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Brain; markedly less than in adenocarcinoma.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.IMMUNE_HYPERSENSITIVITY.notes
Distant lymph nodes.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Smoking-related TMB drives higher neoantigen load. IO responses generally robust. Central location leads to cavitation.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
p40 and p63 are lineage-defining nuclear markers of squamous differentiation. SOX2 amplification maintains basal/stem-cell phenotype. FGFR1 amplification and PIK3CA activation are recurrent alterations in a subset. Distinct biology from lung adenocarcinoma.

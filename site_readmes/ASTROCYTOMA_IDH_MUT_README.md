# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ASTROCYTOMA_IDH_MUT

### metastasis_patterns_note
Primary CNS tumours behave differently from systemic cancers — extraneural metastasis is rare (<2% of gliomas). Spread patterns: local infiltration, and (uncommonly) leptomeningeal/CSF dissemination. Consider reframing this axis as 'pattern of intracranial and neuraxial spread' rather than systemic metastasis.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
IDH mutation produces 2-hydroxyglutarate which reshapes chromatin methylation (G-CIMP phenotype). Vorasidenib is active in grade 2 IDH-mutant diffuse glioma after surgery. Better prognosis than IDH-wildtype glioblastoma.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
IDH mutation is the defining lineage-altering event — 2-HG production reshapes chromatin methylation into a distinct G-CIMP phenotype. Vorasidenib penetrates the CNS and targets mutant IDH1/2 directly. ATRX loss and TP53 mutation are characteristic co-alterations in astrocytoma, IDH-mutant.

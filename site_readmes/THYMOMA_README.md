# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: THYMOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Thymoma typically recurs locally or in pleura rather than by distant haematogenous spread. In a classic series of 126 resected thymomas (Blumberg et al.), 24 recurred; recurrence sites were pleural (22), local (6), and distant (5). Distant metastatic sites when they occur include liver, bone, and lung; breast metastasis is exceptionally rare and limited to case reports. No open-access population-based site-specific frequencies harvested for this pass.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Paraneoplastic autoimmune syndromes are common (myasthenia gravis ~30-50%, PRCA, hypogammaglobulinaemia — Good syndrome). Autoimmune tendency complicates IO use.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Autoimmune paraneoplastic syndromes are characteristically associated with thymoma and reflect retention of thymic function (T-cell selection). GTF2I mutations characterise WHO A and AB subtypes. Distinct from thymic carcinoma which lacks organotypic differentiation.

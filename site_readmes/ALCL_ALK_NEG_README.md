# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: ALCL_ALK_NEG

### heme_involvement_pattern.notes
Typically affects older adults and generally has a worse prognosis than ALK+ ALCL. DUSP22-rearranged subset has been associated with a more favourable outcome than other ALK-negative ALCLs, but not uniformly equivalent to ALK+ ALCL in all series; TP63-rearranged cases have poor outcome. Breast implant-associated ALCL is a distinct entity — usually localised to peri-implant seroma/effusion and capsule.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Typically affects older adults. Molecular subtyping is important — DUSP22-rearranged cases (~20–30%) often have a more favourable prognosis than other ALK-negative ALCLs, whereas TP63-rearranged cases (~8%) have very poor outcomes. BIA-ALCL is a distinct usually localised entity; complete capsulectomy is curative in most localized cases, and systemic therapy is reserved for advanced disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Typically affects older adults. Molecular subtyping is important for prognostication — DUSP22-rearranged disease is generally more favourable than other ALK-negative ALCL, whereas TP63-rearranged disease has very poor prognosis. BIA-ALCL is a distinct entity associated with chronic inflammation around breast implants, especially textured implants; complete surgical excision is curative in most localized cases.

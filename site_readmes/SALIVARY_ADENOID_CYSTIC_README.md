# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SALIVARY_ADENOID_CYSTIC

### metastasis_patterns_note
No population-based source harvested. Clinical: characteristic late distant metastases, most often to lung; perineural spread is characteristic; bone and liver also occur but are less common than lung.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Perineural spread is a defining feature — tumour tracks along nerves. Cold TME explains limited IO responsiveness. Indolent course with late lung metastases.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same MYB-NFIB biology occurs in breast ACC, tracheal ACC, and other adenoid cystic tumours across sites — unified lineage disease. Reduplicated basement membrane material reflects retained myoepithelial function.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: LIP_SCC

### metastasis_patterns_note
Lower-lip vermilion SCC is biologically closer to cutaneous SCC of sun-exposed skin than to intraoral SCC and generally has a low regional and distant metastatic risk; risk increases with advanced T stage, poor differentiation, perineural invasion, and commissure/intraoral extension. Aggregated UAT metastasis data should not be assumed to directly represent vermilion lip SCC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
UV-signature mutation load supports IO responsiveness in advanced/refractory disease.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Biologically closer to cutaneous SCC than to other oral SCCs — UV-driven with high TMB. Distinct from oral cavity SCC despite anatomic proximity.

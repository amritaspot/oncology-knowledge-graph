# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: SCLC

### metastasis_patterns.context
Frequencies among all patients with SCLC (n=22,709 in the SEER cohort). Denominator = all patients with this histology. SCLC has the highest rate of extrathoracic metastasis of any lung cancer subtype.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver; the leading extrathoracic site in SCLC, distinguishing it from all NSCLC subtypes. Liver is the leading single-met site in SCLC (24.4% of single-met cases).

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; highest bone-met rate among all lung histologies.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.NEUROLOGIC.notes
Brain.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.IMMUNE_HYPERSENSITIVITY.notes
Distant lymph nodes.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Rapid growth with paraneoplastic syndromes common (SIADH, ectopic ACTH, LEMS). Molecular subtypes drive emerging therapy approach — SCLC-I shows the greatest relative immunotherapy responsiveness.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
DLL3 is an inhibitory Notch ligand and lineage-associated marker of high-grade pulmonary neuroendocrine tumours (especially SCLC, and also a subset of LCNEC) — target of tarlatamab and previously of rovalpituzumab tesirine. Subtypes drive emerging therapy approach — SCLC-I is relatively more IO responsive. Near-universal TP53 and frequent RB1 inactivation are defining. Origin from PNECs helps explain paraneoplastic hormone syndromes.

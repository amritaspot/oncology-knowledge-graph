# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GEJ_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
See GASTRIC_ADENOCARCINOMA and ESOPHAGEAL_ADENOCARCINOMA — Riihimäki 2018 classifies these under 'Stomach' or 'Esophagus' by anatomic scoring. Behaviour intermediate. Modern staging (AJCC 8) treats GEJ tumours with epicentre within 2 cm of the proximal stomach (cardia) that extend into the GEJ/esophagus as esophageal; tumours with epicentre >2 cm into the proximal stomach are staged as gastric.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Siewert classification determines staging and surgical approach. Higher HER2 rate than distal gastric influences treatment biomarker workflow.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Anatomically defined by Siewert classification (I: distal esophagus, II: cardia straddling, III: proximal stomach). Many GEJ adenocarcinomas, especially Siewert I and some II, are linked to Barrett's metaplasia, whereas Siewert III aligns more with proximal gastric/cardia biology. Higher HER2 expression makes trastuzumab/T-DXd particularly relevant.

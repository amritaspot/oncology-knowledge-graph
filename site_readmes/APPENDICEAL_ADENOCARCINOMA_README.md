# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: APPENDICEAL_ADENOCARCINOMA

### metastasis_patterns_note
Behaves similarly to right-sided colon adenocarcinoma but with substantially greater peritoneal dissemination, especially in mucinous tumors. Peritoneum/omentum are the dominant metastatic sites; ovaries are common in women (Krukenberg-like). Liver metastases occur, particularly in non-mucinous/colonic-type and high-grade tumors, but are less characteristic than peritoneal spread in mucinous appendiceal adenocarcinoma.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Pseudomyxoma peritonei may result from rupture of mucinous appendiceal neoplasms/adenocarcinoma. Cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC is a major treatment approach for selected peritoneal disease. Goblet cell adenocarcinoma has neuroendocrine features but is treated as adenocarcinoma.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Distinct from appendiceal mucinous neoplasm (LAMN/HAMN), which is a lower-grade appendiceal epithelial neoplasm rather than conventional invasive adenocarcinoma. Goblet cell adenocarcinoma is unique to the appendix. Peritoneal spread is characteristic, especially in mucinous tumors.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: PAPILLARY_THYROID

### metastasis_patterns.aggregation_note
diSibio aggregates all thyroid carcinomas. Behaviour differs substantially by subtype: PTC — cervical nodes then lung (miliary/micronodular); FTC — haematogenous, bone and lung; medullary — nodes then liver/lung/bone; anaplastic — rapid local extension and distant.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.IMMUNE_HYPERSENSITIVITY.notes
Lymph nodes (regional + distant); cervical nodes especially prominent in PTC.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.HEPATOBILIARY.notes
Liver; this reflects aggregated thyroid carcinoma autopsy data and is not typical of PTC-specific clinical spread, where liver metastases are uncommon.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.PULMONARY.notes
Lung; miliary/micronodular pulmonary metastases are characteristic of PTC.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.PULMONARY_PLEURA.notes
Pleura.

### metastasis_patterns.organ_involvement.MUSCULOSKELETAL.notes
Bone; less typical for PTC than for FTC clinically.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Hashimoto's thyroiditis association with PTC. Lymphocytic infiltrate associates with better prognosis in some studies. TSH-driven proliferation — TSH suppression is therapeutic.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Aggressive variants (tall cell, columnar, hobnail) share more advanced dedifferentiation. BRAF-driven MAPK activation suppresses NIS expression — MEK inhibition can promote redifferentiation and restore RAI uptake in selected tumors.

# Unstructured Notes & Narrative: GI_NET_G2

### metastasis_patterns_note
Similar tropism to G1 (especially liver and regional nodes; bone can occur, particularly in metastatic midgut disease) but with a higher rate of metastatic burden, visceral spread, and multi-site involvement than G1. Behaviour is intermediate between G1 and G3 well-differentiated NET; distinct from poorly differentiated NEC.

### tumor_microenvironment.notes
Behaviour intermediate between G1 and G3 well-differentiated NET. Same SSTR-driven therapeutic strategy often applies, but progression tends to be faster.

### cellular_states_and_lineage.notes
Same neuroendocrine lineage biology as G1 but with higher proliferation index. SSTR2 expression is often retained, enabling PRRT in appropriate patients. Distinct from poorly differentiated NEC, which has different biology and molecular features.
